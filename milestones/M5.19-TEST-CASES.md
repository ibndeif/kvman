# M5.19 — OpenUI's tables, charts, and lists: test cases

Read: `06` §6.1; ADR 0124, 4, 9, 10, 12, 14, and 16.

The layers: kvwebui's app mounted in happy-dom against the fake API (`test/web/`); kvwebui's built app in Chromium
(`test/e2e/`); the generated reference pages (`test/`); kvcoder's `openui guide` through the test kernel; a real chat,
by hand.

The 19 components of this milestone are: `Table`, `Col`, `EditableTable`; `BarChart`, `HorizontalBarChart`,
`LineChart`, `AreaChart`, `RadarChart`, `Series`, `PieChart`, `RadialChart`, `SingleStackedBarChart`, `Slice`,
`ScatterChart`, `ScatterSeries`, `Point`; `EntityList`, `ListBlock`, `ListItem`.

## Happy path

### M5.19-H1 — a table draws its columns, its cells, and pages of 10 rows
- **Given** `Table([Col("Name", names), Col("Score", scores, "number"), Col("State", @Each(items, "item", Tag(item.state)))])` over 12 rows written in the block
- **When** it is drawn, and the next page is pressed
- **Then** the header has the three labels; the first page has 10 rows, a number column aligned to the inline end in tabular figures, and a `Tag` drawn in each State cell; "1 / 2" shows; Next shows the last 2 rows and "2 / 2"; Previous goes back; the paging works without `onMessage`
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-tables.test.ts`

### M5.19-H2 — `@Sort` and `@Filter` over a bound control change a table and a chart
- **Given** an `items` array, `$sortField` bound to a `Select` and `$search` bound to an `Input`, `sorted = @Sort(@Filter(items, "name", "contains", $search), $sortField, "desc")`, and a `Table` and a `BarChart` drawn from `sorted`
- **When** the person picks another sort field and types in the search box
- **Then** the table's rows and the chart's bars follow the new order and filter
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-tables.test.ts`

### M5.19-H3 — an `EditableTable` edits cells and its save sends the table as Markdown
- **Given** an `EditableTable("prices", [text, number, select, date, url columns], [two rows])` shown with `onMessage`
- **When** the person changes two cells, sees "2 changes", presses Reset, changes one cell again, and presses "Save changes"
- **Then** Reset restores the rows; the save calls `onMessage` with `Save changes`, a blank line, and the table as Markdown (a header row of the columns' headers, a separator, one row per data row, a select cell as its option's label, `|` escaped); the changes bar then shows no change
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-tables.test.ts`

### M5.19-H4 — each chart draws its marks
- **Given** one example of `BarChart` (grouped and stacked), `HorizontalBarChart`, `LineChart` (linear, natural, step), `AreaChart`, `RadarChart`, `PieChart` (pie, donut, semi-circular), `RadialChart`, `SingleStackedBarChart`, and `ScatterChart` with `ScatterSeries` and `Point`s
- **When** each is drawn
- **Then** each is an SVG with `role="img"` and a label naming its kind; bars, lines, areas, a radar polygon, slices, radial bars, stacked segments, and points are drawn, one per value; each series takes the next of `--kv-color-chart-1` to `-8`; a chart with more than one series has a legend; each mark has a `<title>` with its label and value; `xLabel` and `yLabel` are drawn
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-charts.test.ts`

### M5.19-H5 — a pie's slices sum to its circle
- **Given** `PieChart(["A", "B", "C"], [1, 2, 3])`, and the same as a semi-circle
- **When** it is drawn
- **Then** the slices' angles sum to 2π (π for the semi-circle), in proportion 1 : 2 : 3
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-charts.test.ts`

### M5.19-H6 — lists draw their items, and an item with an action sends it
- **Given** an `EntityList` with a header, three rows (one `number`), and a footer; a numbered `ListBlock` of three `ListItem`s, one with `Action([@ToAssistant("Option B")])` and an `actionLabel`; and an image `ListBlock` whose items have a `data:` image and a web image
- **When** they are drawn and the item with an action is pressed, with `onMessage`
- **Then** the rows show left and right (the number in tabular figures, at the inline end); the items are numbered 1 to 3 with their subtitles; only the item with an action is a button, and it sends `Option B`; the `data:` image draws and the web image is a placeholder with its alt text and address; without `onMessage` the item is disabled
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-lists.test.ts`

### M5.19-H7 — the components follow the theme and right to left
- **Given** kvwebui's built app in Chromium with every component of this milestone
- **When** it is shown in light, in dark, and in Arabic
- **Then** no color is pure white or black unless a theme color is; in Arabic a bar chart's first category is at the right and its value axis on the right, while its numbers read left to right; a table's first column is at the right
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/e2e/openui.test.ts`

### M5.19-H8 — the reference names the new components, with their notes
- **Given** the generated pages
- **When** they are read
- **Then** the groups `Tables`, `Charts (2D)`, `Charts (1D)`, and `Charts (Scatter)` have pages in both libraries, `Data Display` names `EntityList`, `ListBlock`, and `ListItem` in the full library, `Lists & Follow-ups` names `ListBlock` and `ListItem` in the chat library; OpenUI's notes for them are there, with an array the UI holds (`items`) in place of a query's rows; every page is what the script writes
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/openui-reference.test.ts`

### M5.19-H9 — `openui guide` gives the new groups
- **Given** a lead chat
- **When** the agent calls `openui guide { place: 'artifact', groups: ['Tables', 'Charts (2D)'] }`
- **Then** it answers those two pages, joined in that order
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/openui-connector.test.ts`

### M5.19-H10 — by hand: a dashboard artifact from a workspace's data
- **Given** a real chat in a workspace with data files
- **When** the person asks for a dashboard of them
- **Then** the lead reads the guide's groups and writes an `openui` artifact with numbers, a table, and three kinds of chart
- Test: by hand (README)

## Edge cases

### M5.19-E1 — a chart with no data, a negative value, or one point draws without an error
- **Given** a `BarChart` with no labels, a `BarChart` with a negative value, a `LineChart` of one point, a `PieChart` of no values, a `PieChart` with a negative value, and a `ScatterChart` with no dataset
- **When** they are drawn
- **Then** nothing throws; the empty ones draw nothing; the negative bar runs below the zero line, which is drawn; the single point is a dot; a negative slice counts as 0
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-charts.test.ts`

### M5.19-E2 — series given as rows are left out, and the rest of the block draws
- **Given** `BarChart(["day", "views", "users"], [["Mon", 100, 50], ["Tue", 200, 75]])` (OpenUI's tabular rows, which come only from a query's results) beside a `TextContent`
- **When** it is drawn
- **Then** OpenUI Lang 0.5 leaves the rows out of `series`, so the chart, with no series, draws nothing; the text draws and nothing throws
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-charts.test.ts`

### M5.19-E3 — an `EditableTable` without `onMessage` can't be edited or saved
- **Given** the table of M5.19-H3 with no `onMessage`
- **When** it is drawn
- **Then** its cells and buttons are disabled
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-tables.test.ts`

### M5.19-E4 — an `EntityList` of size small draws no header or footer
- **Given** `EntityList([rows], "small", header, footer)`
- **When** it is drawn
- **Then** its rows are drawn and its header and footer are not, as OpenUI's description says size small doesn't support them
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-lists.test.ts`

### M5.19-E5 — the pages stay within their sizes, and use no query
- **Given** every generated page
- **When** they are measured and read
- **Then** each index is at most 8 KB, each library's group pages joined at most 30 KB; none has `Query(`, `Mutation(`, `@Run(`, or `data.rows`, and no page names a component of M5.20
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/openui-reference.test.ts`

### M5.19-E6 — the new texts are translated
- **Given** the locale catalogs
- **When** the table's and the editable table's texts are read (Previous page, Next page, the page count, Save changes, Reset, the changes count, each chart's kind)
- **Then** each has an `en` and an `ar` text, and the changes count has its plural forms
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/locales.test.ts`

### M5.19-E7 — the chart colors are defined for light and dark
- **Given** kvwebui's styles
- **When** they are read
- **Then** `--kv-color-chart-1` to `--kv-color-chart-8` are defined for the light theme and again for the dark theme, none pure white or black
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/sources.test.ts`
