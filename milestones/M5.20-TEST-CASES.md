# M5.20 — OpenUI's card blocks, images, modal, carousel, and option cards: test cases

Read: `06` §6.1 and §6.8; ADR 0124, 4, 7, 12, 14, 16, and 19.

The layers: kvwebui's `kvwebui.image.get` through the test kernel (`test/`); kvwebui's app mounted in happy-dom against
the fake API (`test/web/`); kvwebui's built app in Chromium (`test/e2e/`); the generated reference pages (`test/`); a real
chat, by hand.

The 24 components of this milestone are: `SnippetCardBlock`, `SnippetCardItem`, `OverviewCardBlock`,
`OverviewCardItem`, `ContextCardBlock`, `ContextCardItem`, `CompositeCardBlock`, `CompositeCardItem`, `VisualCardBlock`,
`VisualCardItem`, `Text`, `BoldText`, `IconText`, `ImageText`, `ImageTextLarge`, `MetricIndicatorInline`,
`MetricIndicatorWithStrikethrough`; `Image`, `ImageBlock`, `ImageGallery`; `Modal`; `Carousel`; `OptionCards`,
`OptionCard`.

## Happy path

### M5.20-H1 — `kvwebui.image.get` gives a workspace image as base64
- **Given** a workspace with a PNG, a JPEG, a GIF, and a WebP image, one in a folder
- **When** each is asked for by its path relative to the workspace, and the PNG by its absolute path
- **Then** each answers `{ type, data }` with its type read from its first bytes and its whole content as base64; the query is public
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/workspace-image.test.ts`

### M5.20-H2 — the card blocks and their parts draw
- **Given** one block of each kind (`SnippetCardBlock`, `OverviewCardBlock` as a grid and as a carousel, `ContextCardBlock` with a tag title and a gray tint, `CompositeCardBlock` with a header, a body of `Text`, `BoldText`, `MetricIndicatorInline`, `IconText`, `TagBlock`, and a `BarChart`, and a footer with a `MetricIndicatorWithStrikethrough` and a `Button`, and `VisualCardBlock` with a tag)
- **When** they are drawn
- **Then** each draws its cards with their parts: a number in tabular figures read left to right, a `+` metric subtext green and a `-` one red, a trend's arrow and percent, a struck-through previous value, an icon badge in its tone, a context card's Markdown body; a carousel layout scrolls sideways
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-cards.test.ts`

### M5.20-H3 — a card press sends the block's action with the card's label
- **Given** a `SnippetCardBlock` and a `VisualCardBlock` with `Action([@ToAssistant("Tell me more")])`, shown with `onMessage`, and an `OverviewCardBlock` with no action
- **When** the person presses the second snippet card and the first visual card
- **Then** `onMessage` gets `Tell me more`, a blank line, and the snippet card's title, then the same with the visual card's body text; the overview cards are not buttons; without `onMessage` the cards are disabled
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-cards.test.ts`

### M5.20-H4 — images draw from the workspace or `data:`, and nothing else loads
- **Given** an `Image` with a workspace path, an `ImageBlock` with a `data:` image, an `Image` with a web address, an `Image` whose path the query refuses, and an `ImageText` with a workspace path
- **When** they are drawn
- **Then** the workspace images ask `kvwebui.image.get` for their path and draw its answer as a `data:` image; the `data:` image draws; the web address and the refused path are placeholders with the alternative text and the address; no `<img>` has a web address
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-media.test.ts`

### M5.20-H5 — a gallery shows an image larger in a dialog
- **Given** an `ImageGallery` of three `data:` images, one with details
- **When** the person presses that image, then Escape
- **Then** a dialog shows it with its details and its alternative text as the title, and Escape closes it, without `onMessage`
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-media.test.ts`

### M5.20-H6 — a modal opens with `@Set`, closes by Escape, its button, or a press outside, and keeps focus inside
- **Given** `$open = false`, `Button("Edit", Action([@Set($open, true)]))`, and `Modal("Edit", $open, [form])` with a form of two fields, shown with `onMessage`
- **When** the person presses Edit, presses Tab past the last control, presses Escape; opens it again and presses its close button; opens it again and presses outside it
- **Then** the dialog opens with the first control focused; Tab from the last control goes to the first; each of the three closes it and sets `$open` to false (Edit opens it again); focus returns to Edit
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-overlays.test.ts`

### M5.20-H7 — a carousel scrolls by its buttons and keys, mirrored right to left
- **Given** a `Carousel` of three slides, in English and in Arabic
- **When** the person presses Next and Previous, and the arrow keys on the track
- **Then** Next and the Right arrow scroll toward the inline end (rightward in English, leftward in Arabic), Previous and the Left arrow the other way in English, and the arrows swap in Arabic
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-overlays.test.ts`

### M5.20-H8 — option cards are a form field
- **Given** a `Form` with a `FormControl("Plan", OptionCards("plan", "single", [cards with an Icon and an Image on top]))` and a send button, shown with `onMessage`
- **When** the person picks a card, picks another, and sends
- **Then** one card at a time is pressed (`aria-pressed`), the message has `Plan: <the chosen card's title>`; a `multiple` group joins its titles by `, `
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-overlays.test.ts`

### M5.20-H9 — every one of the 86 names is in the reference, within its sizes
- **Given** the generated pages
- **When** they are read
- **Then** every component name of both libraries is in its library's pages, `Cards` has a page in both libraries, each index is at most 8 KB, each library's group pages joined at most 30 KB, and no page has `Query(`, `Mutation(`, `@Run(`, or a web image address
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/openui-reference.test.ts`

### M5.20-H10 — the components follow the theme and right to left in Chromium
- **Given** kvwebui's built app with card blocks, images, a carousel, and option cards
- **When** it is shown in light, in dark, and in Arabic, and the page's requests are watched
- **Then** no color is pure white or black unless a theme color is; the carousel's Next button moves its track leftward in Arabic; no request leaves for any address but kvman's own
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/e2e/openui.test.ts`

### M5.20-H11 — by hand: a gallery, option cards, and KPI cards
- **Given** a real chat in a workspace with screenshots
- **When** the person asks to see the screenshots and to choose between options, and for an overview of numbers
- **Then** the lead shows the screenshots in a gallery and the options as option cards in a reply, and the numbers as card blocks in an artifact
- Test: by hand (README)

## Edge cases

### M5.20-E1 — a path that leaves the workspace is refused
- **Given** a workspace and an image outside it
- **When** `kvwebui.image.get` is asked for `../outside.png`, the outside image's absolute path, and a link inside the workspace that points to it
- **Then** each fails `VALIDATION_FAILED`
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/workspace-image.test.ts`

### M5.20-E2 — an SVG or any other file is refused, whatever its name
- **Given** an SVG named `logo.svg`, an SVG named `logo.png`, a text file, and a folder
- **When** each is asked for
- **Then** each fails `VALIDATION_FAILED`
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/workspace-image.test.ts`

### M5.20-E3 — a missing image and one over 5 MiB
- **Given** no `missing.png`, and a PNG of 5 MiB plus one byte
- **When** each is asked for
- **Then** the first fails `NOT_FOUND`, the second `TOO_LARGE` with `params.limit` 5242880
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/workspace-image.test.ts`

### M5.20-E4 — a modal with no binding never opens, and a block streaming one stays closed
- **Given** `Modal("Note", null, [text])`
- **When** it is drawn
- **Then** no dialog is shown and nothing throws
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-overlays.test.ts`

### M5.20-E5 — the new texts are translated
- **Given** the locale catalogs
- **When** the dialog's Close, the carousel's name and its Previous and Next, and `kvwebui.image.get`'s description are read
- **Then** each has an `en` and an `ar` text
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/locales.test.ts`

### M5.20-E6 — an argument skipped with `null` is left out
- **Given** `VisualCardItem(BoldText("text", "Room"), "v1", null, Tag("New"))` in a block, and `Image("A photo", null)`
- **When** they are drawn
- **Then** nothing throws; the card draws with no image and its tag; the image is a placeholder with its alternative text alone
- Test: `extensions/kvwebui/test/web/openui-media.test.ts`
