# M5.4 — Composing and the player: test cases

Read: `16` §16.7, §16.8; `04` §4.1; ADR 0107, 6, 8, 12 to 14, 23, and 24.

The layers: each scene's length, the ASS file, a font's family, each scene's and the join's ffmpeg arguments, and the byte ranges as unit tests; `compose` through the test kernel with stand-in `ffmpeg` and `ffprobe` that record their arguments and write their outputs; the file route with a real kvman child; the card in `happy-dom`; and one composition with the ffmpeg on the PATH, checked with ffprobe (`real-ffmpeg.test.ts`). Last, the by-hand check with a real ffmpeg.

## Happy path

### M5.4-H1 — a scene's length
- **Given** scenes: a video part with a longer narration; a video part with a shorter narration; an image with a narration; an image with `durationMs`; a video with `durationMs` and a narration; a video alone, cut with `from` and `to`
- **When** their lengths are found
- **Then** they are the narration's, the narration's, the narration's, the `durationMs`, the `durationMs`, and `to − from` (cut to the clip's end); the video's own part is held or cut to that
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-plan.test.ts`

### M5.4-H2 — a scene's ffmpeg arguments
- **Given** a 1280 × 720 composition; a video scene from 2000 to 5000 ms with a narration and captions; an image scene of 3000 ms without narration
- **When** each scene's arguments are made
- **Then** the video is read from 2 s for 3 s, the image looped at 30 frames a second; each is scaled to fit on black, at 30 frames a second, in `yuv420p`, held with its last frame, with the ASS file burned in through `subtitles` and the fonts folder; the sound is the narration padded with silence, or silence (`anullsrc`, 48 kHz stereo); both encode `libx264 -preset medium -crf 20` and AAC 160 kb/s 48 kHz stereo, cut to the scene's length
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-args.test.ts`

### M5.4-H3 — the join
- **Given** three parts
- **When** the join's list and arguments are made
- **Then** the list names each part in order, and ffmpeg joins them with the concat demuxer, `-c copy`, and `+faststart`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-args.test.ts`

### M5.4-H4 — the ASS file
- **Given** captions in English and Arabic, at the bottom and at the top, with the default size and with 40 px
- **When** a scene's ASS file is made at 1280 × 720
- **Then** its `PlayResX`/`PlayResY` are the size; its style is the font's family, white with a black outline of 3 px, with the encoding -1 so each line takes its own direction, centered at the bottom (`2`) or top (`8`) with a margin of 6% of the height, at the height ÷ 18 (40) px or the given size; each caption is a `Dialogue` with times as `h:mm:ss.cc`; `{`, `}`, and `\` are left out of the text
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/ass-file.test.ts`

### M5.4-H5 — the font
- **Given** captions without a font, once with an Arabic letter and once without; a bundled name; a `.ttf` in the workspace
- **When** the font is chosen
- **Then** it is IBM Plex Sans Arabic, then Poppins; the bundled one; the workspace file's own family, copied into the fonts folder; every bundled file names its own family
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/caption-font.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/font-family.test.ts`

### M5.4-H6 — compose through the kernel
- **Given** a stand-in ffprobe that gives each file a length, and a stand-in ffmpeg that writes each part and the joined file
- **When** `compose` runs with two scenes to `videos/tour.mp4`
- **Then** ffmpeg ran once per scene and once to join; the file is the joined output; the answer is `{ file, durationMs, bytes, fileId }` with the sum of the scenes' lengths; the temporary folder is gone
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose.test.ts`

### M5.4-H7 — the player's copies
- **Given** compositions to `a.mp4`, then `a.mp4` again with `replace`, then ten more files
- **When** they finish
- **Then** each has a copy in the kernel's files, `video/mp4`; the second `a.mp4` replaces the first copy; the eleventh path removes the oldest copy; `players` holds the newest 10
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose.test.ts`

### M5.4-H8 — the file route answers a range
- **Given** a file of 1000 bytes
- **When** it is fetched with `Range: bytes=0-99`, `bytes=900-`, `bytes=-100`, and none
- **Then** the first three are 206 with their bytes, `Content-Length`, and `Content-Range: bytes <first>-<last>/1000`; the last is 200 with the whole file; every answer has `Accept-Ranges: bytes`, the attachment, and `nosniff`
- Test: `packages/cli/test/http/files.test.ts`

### M5.4-H9 — the card plays the copy
- **Given** a successful `compose` with `fileId`, in the workspace `w1`
- **When** the card is mounted
- **Then** it shows the path and the length as `m:ss`, and a `<video>` with controls and `preload="metadata"` from `/api/files/<fileId>?workspaceId=w1`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/web/player.test.ts`

### M5.4-H10 — a real composition
- **Given** the ffmpeg on the PATH, a recorded clip, an image, a WAV narration made with ffmpeg, and English and Arabic captions
- **When** `compose` runs at 640 × 360
- **Then** ffprobe reads H.264 640 × 360 at 30 frames a second and AAC 48 kHz stereo, as long as the answer's `durationMs` within 100 ms
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/real-ffmpeg.test.ts`

### M5.4-H11 — the connector has compose with its card
- **Given** kvvideo loaded
- **When** the connector list is read
- **Then** `video` has `compose` → `kvvideo.video.compose`, a command, with an example and `card: 'kvvideo.player'`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose.test.ts`

## Edge cases

### M5.4-E1 — an image without a length
- **Given** an image scene without narration or `durationMs`
- **When** `compose` runs
- **Then** it fails `VALIDATION_FAILED` before ffmpeg runs; `from` or `to` on an image fails too, and `to` not after `from`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-plan.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose.test.ts`

### M5.4-E2 — input limits
- **Given** the compose schema
- **When** it reads 0 or 101 scenes, an odd width, a size of 318 or 1922, 201 captions, a caption of 201 characters or with `endMs` not after `startMs`, a `durationMs` of 99 or 600 001, a caption size of 11 or 201, an unknown position, and unknown keys
- **Then** each fails `VALIDATION_FAILED`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/compose-input.test.ts`

### M5.4-E3 — files that aren't there or aren't the right kind
- **Given** a scene clip that doesn't exist, a clip `.gif`, a narration `.ogg`, an output `.mov`, an existing output without `replace`, and a path outside the workspace
- **When** `compose` runs
- **Then** `NOT_FOUND` for the missing file and `VALIDATION_FAILED` for the others, before ffmpeg runs
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose.test.ts`

### M5.4-E4 — an unknown font
- **Given** `captions.font` of `Comic Sans`, a missing `fonts/x.ttf`, and a file that isn't a font
- **When** `compose` runs
- **Then** the name fails `VALIDATION_FAILED` listing the bundled names; the missing file `NOT_FOUND`; the bad file `VALIDATION_FAILED`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/caption-font.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/font-family.test.ts`

### M5.4-E5 — ffmpeg fails on a scene, or on the join
- **Given** a stand-in ffmpeg that fails on the second scene, then one that fails on the join
- **When** `compose` runs
- **Then** it fails `kvvideo/RENDER_FAILED { scene: 2, reason }`, then `{ scene: null, reason }`, with ffmpeg's last lines; nothing is written; the temporary folder is gone
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose.test.ts`

### M5.4-E6 — ffprobe can't read an input
- **Given** a stand-in ffprobe that fails for a clip
- **When** `compose` runs
- **Then** it fails `kvvideo/RENDER_FAILED { scene, reason }` with that scene
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose.test.ts`

### M5.4-E7 — cancel stops ffmpeg and removes the temporary folder
- **Given** a stand-in ffmpeg that never ends
- **When** the compose job is cancelled
- **Then** the job ends `cancelled`, ffmpeg is stopped, nothing is written, and the temporary folder is gone
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose.test.ts`

### M5.4-E8 — a large file has no copy
- **Given** files of 512 MiB and of 512 MiB and one byte
- **When** kvvideo decides whether to copy them (a real file that large would fill a RAM-backed `/tmp`, so the rule is tested alone)
- **Then** the first is copied, and the second isn't, so its `fileId` is `null`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/player-copies.test.ts`

### M5.4-E9 — ranges that can't be served
- **Given** a file of 1000 bytes
- **When** it is fetched with `bytes=1000-`, `bytes=-0`, two ranges, `bytes=9-1`, and `items=0-9`
- **Then** the first two are 416 with `Content-Range: bytes */1000` and no body; the others are 200 with the whole file
- Test: `packages/kernel/test/http/byte-range.test.ts`, `packages/cli/test/http/files.test.ts`

### M5.4-E10 — the card without a copy
- **Given** a `compose` output with `fileId: null`, then one whose video fails to load
- **When** the card is mounted, in `en` and `ar`
- **Then** it shows "This video can't play here. Open `<file>` in the workspace." (and its Arabic) in place of the video
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/web/player.test.ts`

### M5.4-E11 — caption paths in ffmpeg's filter
- **Given** a scene with captions, in a work folder such as `C:\Users\O'Brien\AppData\Local\Temp\kvvideo-a`
- **When** its arguments are made
- **Then** the `subtitles` filter names `scene-<n>.ass` and `fonts` relative to the work folder, where ffmpeg runs, so no path is written into the filter
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-args.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose.test.ts`
