# M5.17 — Craft notes for better videos: test cases

Read: `16` §16.1, §16.3, §16.4, and §16.12; ADR 0121.

The layers: `video craft` through the test kernel and kvcoder's `runConnector`; the connector's entry in `kvcoder.connector.list`; the guide in the prompt after `/video`; the notes and the guide as files of the package; two videos made with `/video`, by hand.

## Happy path

### M5.17-H1 — `video craft` answers each note
- **Given** kvvideo started
- **When** `video craft` runs with each of `story`, `scenes`, `motion`, `charts`, `tutorial`, and `social`
- **Then** each answers `{ content }`, the text of `docs/craft/<topic>.md`, which is not empty and starts with its title
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/craft.test.ts`

### M5.17-H2 — `craft` is a query of the connector, with an example
- **Given** kvvideo started
- **When** the connectors are listed
- **Then** `video` has `craft`, running `kvvideo.craft.get` as a query (so a read-only worker's helper has it, plan 08 §8.5), after `brand`, with one example
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/registration.test.ts`

### M5.17-H3 — the guide reads each note at its stage
- **Given** a chat after `/video`
- **When** its prompt is read
- **Then** the Concept stage names `video craft` with `story`, `tutorial`, and `social`; the Treatment stage names `scenes`, `motion`, and `charts`, a scene's pattern, and a verb per element; the Pictures stage tells a designer to read `scenes` and `motion` before building; the review names each fault with its time and what to change; and the production rules name the held key moment, numbers written as said, a second element for the eye, and `camera: 'none'`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/registration.test.ts`

### M5.17-H4 — the notes ship with the package
- **Given** kvvideo's package
- **When** its files are read
- **Then** each of the six notes is in `docs/craft/`, and the package's `files` holds `docs`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/craft.test.ts`

### M5.17-H5 — by hand: an explainer with a chart and a tutorial
- **Given** a workspace with an app
- **When** an explainer with a chart, and a tutorial of the app, are made with `/video`
- **Then** the lead reads `story`, `scenes`, `motion`, and `charts` (and `tutorial` for the tutorial) at their stages, and the designers read `scenes` and `motion`
- Test: by hand (README)

## Edge cases

### M5.17-E1 — another topic, or none, fails
- **Given** kvvideo started
- **When** `kvvideo.craft.get` runs with the topic `music`, with no topic, or with another field beside the topic
- **Then** each fails `VALIDATION_FAILED`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/craft.test.ts`

### M5.17-E2 — each note within 8 KB, and the guide within a section
- **Given** the six notes and the guide
- **When** their sizes are measured in bytes
- **Then** each note is at most 8 KB, and the guide at most 16 KB, a section's limit (plan 08 §8.4)
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/craft.test.ts`

### M5.17-E3 — the guide no longer crossfades every scene
- **Given** the guide
- **When** it is read
- **Then** it doesn't say "Use `crossfade` between scenes" or "one focal point", nor tell the lead to give a designer "the motion rules below"
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/registration.test.ts`
