# M5.6 — A narration that sounds directed and lines up: test cases

Read: `16` §16.2, §16.6; ADR 0115, 15, 17, 39 to 41.

The layers: the sentences and lines, the captions' times (in proportion and from character times), the pronunciations, the service requests and answers, the join's arguments, and the cache's trimming as unit tests; `narrate` through the test kernel with stand-in `piper`, `ffmpeg`, and `ffprobe` programs (a sentence's audio lasts one second per word, and the joined file the sum with 250 ms between sentences). The two web services are tested with a stand-in `fetch` only, since no test reaches the network; their real behaviour is part of the by-hand check of M5.11.

## Happy path

### M5.6-H1 — three sentences are said one by one and joined with 250 ms between them
- **Given** Piper, and the text `Open the app. Sign in now. Write a note.`
- **When** `narrate` runs
- **Then** Piper ran three times, once per sentence; ffmpeg joined the three files with the join's arguments; `durationMs` is 9500; the first caption of each sentence starts where its audio starts (0, 3250, and 6500 ms); the sidecar beside the file has `kind: 'narration'`, the length, and the captions; `reused` is 0
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/narrate.test.ts`

### M5.6-H2 — the join's arguments
- **Given** three sentence files
- **When** the join's arguments are made
- **Then** each input is brought to 44.1 kHz mono, the first two padded with 0.25 s, the three joined in order, and encoded with `libmp3lame` at 192k; one file has no padding
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/join-args.test.ts`

### M5.6-H3 — ElevenLabs's request: its address, neighbours, and speed
- **Given** a sentence with one before and one after it
- **When** its ElevenLabs request is made at speed 1, then at 0.9
- **Then** the address ends in `/with-timestamps?output_format=mp3_44100_128`; the body has `text`, `model_id`, `previous_text`, and `next_text`, and `voice_settings: { speed: 0.9 }` only at 0.9; the first sentence has no `previous_text` and the last no `next_text`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/speech-services.test.ts`

### M5.6-H4 — ElevenLabs's answer gives the audio and the character times
- **Given** an answer `{ audio_base64, alignment }` for `Hi there.`
- **When** it is read
- **Then** the audio is the decoded bytes and each character has its start and end in whole milliseconds
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/speech-services.test.ts`

### M5.6-H5 — OpenAI's request has the tone and the speed
- **When** an OpenAI request is made with `tone` and speed 1.1, then with neither
- **Then** the first body has `instructions` equal to the tone and `speed: 1.1`; the second has neither
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/speech-services.test.ts`

### M5.6-H6 — Piper's speed is its length scale
- **Given** Piper
- **When** `narrate` runs with `speed: 0.8`
- **Then** Piper got `--length-scale 1.25`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/narrate.test.ts`

### M5.6-H7 — lines and words from the character times
- **Given** a sentence of two lines with character times, placed at 1000 ms
- **When** its captions are made
- **Then** each line starts at its first character's time plus 1000, each word runs from its first character's start to its last character's end, and each line ends where the next starts; the last ends at the narration's length
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/captions.test.ts`

### M5.6-H8 — lines in proportion within each sentence
- **Given** two sentences without character times, at 0 (2000 ms long) and 2250 (1000 ms long)
- **When** their captions are made
- **Then** each sentence's lines share its own length by their characters, the second sentence's first line starts at 2250, the first sentence's last line ends at 2250, and no line has words
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/captions.test.ts`

### M5.6-H9 — a pronunciation changes what is said, not the captions, and keeps its times
- **Given** the list `[{ word: 'kvman', say: 'kay vee man' }]`
- **When** `Open KVMAN now.` and `kvmanual stays.` are spoken
- **Then** the first is said `Open kay vee man now.`, each character of `KVMAN` spans the whole replacement, and the others keep their places; `kvmanual` is unchanged; with character times the word `KVMAN` runs from `kay`'s start to `man`'s end, and the caption reads `KVMAN`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/pronunciations.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/captions.test.ts`

### M5.6-H10 — the cache: the same text again is free, a changed sentence alone is said again
- **Given** a narration of three sentences already made
- **When** the same text is narrated to another file, then a text with its second sentence changed
- **Then** the second call runs Piper no time and answers `reused: 3` with the same captions; the third runs Piper once and answers `reused: 2`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/narrate.test.ts`

### M5.6-H11 — the cache keeps the newest 2000 rows
- **Given** a cache trimmed to a limit of 3
- **When** a fifth row is kept
- **Then** the two oldest rows are deleted with their files, and the three newest stay
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/narration-cache.test.ts`

### M5.6-H12 — the cache key
- **When** keys are made for the same sentence with another voice, model, tone, speed, spoken text, or service
- **Then** each differs, and the same inputs give the same key
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/narration-cache.test.ts`

### M5.6-H13 — the pronunciations setting, its row, and the progress
- **Given** kvvideo started
- **When** its settings, its extension page, and a narration's progress are read
- **Then** `kvvideo.narration.pronunciations` defaults to `[]` in the global and workspace scopes; the Narration card has its row; narrating three sentences sends `kvvideo.progress.sentence` with `n` 1 to 3 and `of: 3`; both keys have `en` and `ar` texts
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/configuration.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/narrate.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/catalog.test.ts`

## Edge cases

### M5.6-E1 — a failing sentence fails the call, and nothing is written
- **Given** a Piper that fails on the second sentence
- **When** `narrate` runs
- **Then** it fails `kvvideo/NARRATION_FAILED` with Piper's last lines; no file or sidecar is written; the first sentence stays in the cache
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/narrate.test.ts`

### M5.6-E2 — ElevenLabs character times that don't match the text, or an answer without audio
- **When** an answer's characters spell another text, or have another count of times, or the answer has no `audio_base64`
- **Then** each fails `kvvideo/NARRATION_FAILED`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/speech-services.test.ts`

### M5.6-E3 — ElevenLabs without character times
- **When** an answer's `alignment` is `null`
- **Then** the audio is read and the sentence's lines are in proportion
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/speech-services.test.ts`

### M5.6-E4 — tone and speed limits, and a text with nothing to say
- **When** `narrate` gets `speed: 0.69`, `speed: 1.21`, a tone of 301 characters, an empty tone, or a text of spaces
- **Then** each fails `VALIDATION_FAILED` before a service is called
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/narrate.test.ts`

### M5.6-E5 — a pronunciation entry beyond its limits
- **When** the setting is set to a word of 101 characters, an empty `say`, 201 entries, or an entry with another field
- **Then** each fails `VALIDATION_FAILED`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/configuration.test.ts`

### M5.6-E6 — an existing sidecar is replaced only with replace
- **Given** `a.mp3.kvvideo.json` without `a.mp3`
- **When** `narrate` writes `a.mp3` without `replace`, then with it
- **Then** the first fails `VALIDATION_FAILED` before Piper runs; the second writes both
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/narrate.test.ts`

### M5.6-E7 — a word of the list inside a longer word, or in another case, or with regex characters
- **When** `C++` and `kvman` are in the list, and `Use c++ and xkvman.` is spoken
- **Then** `c++` is replaced and `xkvman` isn't
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/pronunciations.test.ts`
