# M5.7 — The look: camera, frames, overlays, captions, and the brand: test cases

Read: `16` §16.2, §16.4, §16.8 (Scenes, the camera, an image's camera, the frame, overlays, captions); ADR 0115, 7, 10, 11, 18, 21, 34, 36, 42, 43, 58, and 60.

The layers: the camera's stops and expressions, an image's zoom, the frame's layout, the background's inputs, the overlays' filters, the ASS file in each style, the brand's colours, and the scenes' defaults as unit tests; `compose`, `brand`, and `brand-set` through the test kernel with the stand-in ffmpeg and ffprobe, which log each run's arguments and filter graph file; the drawn images with the installed Chrome; and a real render with the ffmpeg on the PATH whose frames are read back.

## Happy path

### M5.7-H1 — the camera stops at each click and type, and eases between them
- **Given** a recording of 1280 × 720 at 2× with a click at (100, 80, 40 × 20) acting 600 ms after it starts, a type at (900, 500, 200 × 30) starting 1.5 s after the click ends, and a click 5 s later
- **When** the camera is planned
- **Then** the view starts whole; eases to 1.6× around the first box from its `atMs` to its `actMs`; pans to the second over 600 ms from its `atMs`; holds, then eases out over 800 ms from 1.5 s after the second's `endMs`; and eases in again for the third; each view is kept inside the page
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/camera.test.ts`

### M5.7-H2 — a large box zooms less, and never below 1×
- **When** the stops for boxes of 800 × 100 and 1280 × 720 are planned in a 1280 × 720 page
- **Then** their zooms are 0.8 × 1280 ÷ 800 = 1.28 and 1
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/camera.test.ts`

### M5.7-H3 — the camera as perspective expressions, by frame
- **Given** a camera plan and a part starting 2000 ms into the recording
- **When** its `perspective` filter is made
- **Then** it is evaluated each frame, its time is `in/30` plus 2, and at sample times its corners (evaluated in TypeScript from the same plan) bound the planned view at 2×
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/camera.test.ts`

### M5.7-H4 — an image's slow zoom
- **When** an image scene of 4000 ms with `camera: 'auto'` is planned
- **Then** its view goes from 1× to 1.08× at an even pace, centred
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/camera.test.ts`

### M5.7-H5 — the window, the phone, and none, laid out
- **Given** a 1920 × 1080 picture and a 1280 × 720 recording
- **When** a window is laid out with captions at the bottom, at the top, and with no captions; a phone with a 390 × 844 recording; and none
- **Then** the window keeps the recording's shape inside margins of 64.8 px (6% of 1080) and 151.2 px (14%) at the bottom with bottom captions, centred; the phone is 950.4 px high (88%) with its screen inside its outline; none fits the clip, centred
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/frame-layout.test.ts`

### M5.7-H6 — the background: an image, a colour, the brand's gradient, the default gradient
- **When** the background inputs are made for an image, `#112233`, a brand of background `#ffffff` and primary `#0066ff`, and no brand
- **Then** an image is scaled to cover and cropped; a colour is a gradient from it to its mix with 18% of the primary (or itself without a brand); the brand gives `#ffffff` to `#d1e3ff`; no brand gives `#0F172A` to `#1E293B`; each gradient has no motion
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/background.test.ts`

### M5.7-H7 — overlays on the page: a callout fades, a highlight and a blur appear, all on their boxes at 2×
- **Given** a recording's sidecar whose step 2 has the box (100, 50, 200 × 40)
- **When** a callout on step 2 from 1000 to 3000 ms, a highlight on a box, and a blur on step 2 are made into filters
- **Then** the callout's image fades in at 1 s and out at 2.8 s over 0.2 s and is shown between 1 and 3 s; the highlight's image is shown in its time; the blur crops (200, 100, 400 × 80) at 2×, blurs it with a strength of 20, and lays it back in its time
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/overlays.test.ts`

### M5.7-H8 — the drawn images: a window's shadow and its mask, a phone, a callout, and a highlight
- **Given** the installed Chrome
- **When** a window's chrome and mask, a phone's chrome, a callout, and a highlight are drawn
- **Then** each is a PNG of its size; the window's mask is white inside its rounded corners and black outside; the chrome is clear where the background shows and dark under the shadow; the callout's label is in the brand's primary colour; the highlight is dark outside the box and clear inside it
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/drawn-images.test.ts`

### M5.7-H9 — the caption styles
- **Given** captions, one with words
- **When** the ASS file is made as `boxed`, `outline`, and `highlight`
- **Then** `boxed` is an opaque box (`BorderStyle` 3) in black at 65% with padding of a third of the size; `outline` is today's outline of 3 px; `highlight` adds a style whose primary colour is the brand's (or `#FFD54A`) and whose secondary is white, and writes `\k` times for each word of a line with words, while a line without words uses the boxed style
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/ass-file.test.ts`

### M5.7-H10 — a scene's captions from its narration's sidecar, and the brand's font
- **Given** a narration with a sidecar of two caption lines, and a brand whose font is Lato
- **When** a scene with that narration and no captions is composed
- **Then** its ASS file has the sidecar's two lines in Lato
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose-look.test.ts`

### M5.7-H11 — the scenes' defaults from their clips
- **Given** a recording of a website on a desktop, one on a phone, a recorded page, an image, and a clip without a sidecar
- **When** each is planned with no `frame` or `camera`
- **Then** they are window and auto, phone and auto, none and none, none and auto, and none and none
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-plan.test.ts`

### M5.7-H12 — the brand, read and set
- **Given** a workspace with `brand/logo.png`
- **When** `brand-set` sets the logo, colours, and Lato, then `brand` reads it
- **Then** `brand` answers what was set, and the workspace's `kvvideo.brand` holds it; the Brand card is on the extension page
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/brand.test.ts`

### M5.7-H13 — compose's default size and its graph file
- **Given** an image scene
- **When** it is composed with no size
- **Then** the picture is 1920 × 1080, and ffmpeg got its graph with `-filter_complex_script`, a file in the work folder
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose-look.test.ts`

### M5.7-H14 — a real render with a window, the camera, a blur, a callout, and boxed captions
- **Given** the ffmpeg on the PATH and Chrome, and a recording of a page with a click
- **When** it is composed at 640 × 360 with its defaults, a blur, a callout, and a caption
- **Then** the video is 640 × 360; a frame from before the click shows the default gradient's colour in its corner and the page in the window; a frame at the click is zoomed in (the page's area is larger than at the start)
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/real-look.test.ts`

### M5.7-H15 — check needs the new filters
- **Given** an ffmpeg without `gradients`, `perspective`, `alphamerge`, `xfade`, `loudnorm`, and `afftdn`
- **When** `check` runs
- **Then** `missing` names them after `subtitles`, in the plan's order
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/setup.test.ts`

## Edge cases

### M5.7-E1 — an overlay placed by both, by neither, by a step without a box, or a callout without text
- **When** a scene has an overlay with `step` and `box`; with neither; on a step without a box (a `wait`); on a step past the recording's; a callout without `text`; `toMs` not after `fromMs`
- **Then** each fails `VALIDATION_FAILED` before ffmpeg runs
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose-look.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/compose-input.test.ts`

### M5.7-E2 — `step` on a clip without a sidecar
- **When** an overlay names a step of a clip with no sidecar
- **Then** it fails `VALIDATION_FAILED`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose-look.test.ts`

### M5.7-E3 — a page and a video clip are never moved, `camera: 'none'` neither
- **When** a recorded page and a clip with `camera: 'auto'`, and a recording with `camera: 'none'`, are planned
- **Then** none has a camera
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-plan.test.ts`

### M5.7-E4 — captions off
- **When** a scene with captions is composed with `captions.style: 'off'`
- **Then** no ASS file is written and its graph has no `subtitles`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose-look.test.ts`

### M5.7-E5 — a brand beyond its rules
- **When** `brand-set` gets a logo outside the workspace, a logo that is a `.gif`, a missing logo, a colour that isn't `#RRGGBB`, and an unknown font
- **Then** they fail `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `NOT_FOUND`, `VALIDATION_FAILED`, and `VALIDATION_FAILED`, and the setting is unchanged
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/brand.test.ts`

### M5.7-E6 — a frame of window or phone on a clip without a sidecar is measured
- **Given** a clip without a sidecar that ffprobe says is 1000 × 500
- **When** it is composed with `frame: 'window'`
- **Then** ffprobe was asked for its size, and the window has its 2:1 shape
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose-look.test.ts`

### M5.7-E7 — a background image outside the workspace or of another kind
- **When** a scene's `background` is `../bg.png`, `bg.gif`, or `#12345`
- **Then** each fails `VALIDATION_FAILED`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose-look.test.ts`
