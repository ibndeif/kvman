# QA 8 — a reply that vanishes (ADR 0009, 187–190)

Found by asking the agent to continue: it said it would write the files, the turn ended, and "continue" did it again. The raw stream showed the provider generating the file-writing call and never delivering it; all seven lost replies were `fs write` calls with JSON-escaped content, while `cat` heredocs and a raw heredoc body arrived. The product owner chose the raw body form, a recovery for lost replies, and a stream chunk that never carries a value over 16 KiB. Every scenario names its test. Backend tests run a real turn against the fake model (which can report `usage` without content, as the provider did); `test/web/` mounts components in happy-dom; the kvai ones are in `extensions/kvai/test/`.

## Happy path

- **QA8-H1 `fs write` takes its content as the heredoc body.** *Given* `fs write '{"path":"a/index.html"}'` and a heredoc of three lines with quotes, backslashes, and `$HOME`, *then* the file holds exactly those lines with a final line break, its folders are created, and the result is `{ "path", "created": true, "bytes" }`. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/fs-body.test.ts`)
- **QA8-H2 `artifact write` takes its content as the heredoc body.** *Given* `artifact write '{"id":"design","title":"Design","format":"html"}'` and a heredoc holding a page with a script, *then* `kvcoder.artifact.get` returns that page with a final line break, and the title and format. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/artifact-body.test.ts`)
- **QA8-H3 The PowerShell here-string works the same.** *Given* the `@' … '@ | fs write '{"path":…}'` form, *then* the file holds the body. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/write-body.test.ts`)
- **QA8-H4 The JSON-only form still works.** (`extensions/kvcoder/test/fs-connector.test.ts`, `extensions/kvcoder/test/artifact-connector.test.ts`)
- **QA8-H5 The help shows the body form.** *Then* `fs -h` and `artifact -h` show a `write` with the body first, and the index entries of `fs` and `artifact` and the base prompt's execute step say to give content as the raw heredoc body, one file per call. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/prompt.test.ts`, `extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/prompt-build.test.ts`, `extensions/kvcoder/test/run-connector.test.ts`)
- **QA8-H6 A lost reply gets a hint and another step.** *Given* a model reply with 30 characters of text, no call, and `usage.output` 3 400, followed by a reply that writes the file, *then* the turn doesn't end after the first: a message from `@kvman/kvcoder` follows it, naming about 3 400 tokens, the second step runs the call, and the turn ends `done`. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/lost-reply.test.ts`)
- **QA8-H7 The hint is the model's, and the person sees a card.** *Then* the hint is stored as a `user` message with `source: { kind: 'extension', name: '@kvman/kvcoder' }` and is in the next model request; the conversation shows it as a muted card labelled `kvcoder told the model`, not as the person's bubble. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/lost-reply.test.ts`, `extensions/kvcoder/test/web/lost-reply-card.test.ts`)
- **QA8-H8 After two retries the turn ends with a notice.** *Given* three lost replies in a row, *then* the third ends the turn `failed` with the notice `REPLY_LOST` whose params hold the tokens, no fourth step runs, and `en` and `ar` translate it. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/lost-reply.test.ts`, `extensions/kvcoder/test/locales.test.ts`)
- **QA8-H9 The stream chunk never carries a value over 16 KiB.** *Given* a tool call whose `command` is 20 KB, *then* the `toolcall` chunk that completes it has `command: null` and its other arguments as they were, while the returned message holds the whole command. (`extensions/kvai/test/toolcall-arguments.test.ts`)
- **QA8-H10 A call over 64 KiB no longer fails its step.** *Given* a call whose `command` is 70 KB, *then* the step runs the call. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/big-call.test.ts`)

## Edge cases

- **QA8-E1 `content` twice fails.** *Given* a JSON argument with `content` and a heredoc, *then* `error VALIDATION_FAILED: Give the content once…` and nothing is written. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/write-body.test.ts`)
- **QA8-E2 The JSON argument must be an object.** *Given* an argument that isn't JSON, or is an array, with a heredoc, *then* `VALIDATION_FAILED`. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/write-body.test.ts`)
- **QA8-E3 Other commands still take their JSON once.** *Given* `fs edit '{…}'` with a heredoc, or `ask text '{…}'` with a heredoc, *then* `Give the JSON input once: as an argument or on stdin.` as before. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/write-body.test.ts`)
- **QA8-E4 An empty body is an empty content.** *Given* an `fs write` with an empty heredoc, *then* an empty file; an empty `artifact write` fails `VALIDATION_FAILED` (an artifact's content is never empty). (`extensions/kvcoder/test/fs-body.test.ts`, `extensions/kvcoder/test/artifact-body.test.ts`)
- **QA8-E5 A body that looks like JSON is still just text.** *Given* a heredoc whose body is `{"a": 1}`, *then* the file holds those characters. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/fs-body.test.ts`)
- **QA8-E6 An ordinary reply is never lost.** *Given* a short answer, a long Arabic answer, an answer with thinking, and a reply that has a call, with their real token counts, *then* none is flagged and the turn ends or goes on as before. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/lost-reply.test.ts`, `extensions/kvcoder/test/lost-reply.test.ts`)
- **QA8-E7 The thresholds are exact.** *Given* 150 tokens unexplained by the text and the reasoning, *then* not lost; 151, lost; reasoning tokens as the provider reports them are subtracted, with half the visible thinking's characters standing in when it reports none; and a reply with 400 characters of text is no longer short, only one under 400 is eligible. (ADR 0009, 191)
- **QA8-E14 Real replies are classified as they were.** *Given* the usage and text of the twelve no-call replies in the product owner's chats, *then* the five genuine final answers are not lost and the four lost ones are. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/lost-reply.test.ts`) (`extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/lost-reply.test.ts`)
- **QA8-E8 The retry count belongs to the turn.** *Given* two lost replies in a turn, then a new message and a turn with one lost reply, *then* that one gets its retry. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/lost-reply.test.ts`)
- **QA8-E9 A lost reply counts as a step.** *Given* `kvcoder.maxSteps` 2 and a lost reply, *then* the second step is the last and a further one ends the turn `maxSteps`. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/lost-reply.test.ts`)
- **QA8-E10 A cancelled turn is not retried.** *Given* the person stops the turn while the hint is queued, *then* the turn ends `cancelled`, as ever. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/lost-reply.test.ts`)
- **QA8-E11 A subagent's lost reply is retried too.** (`extensions/kvcoder/test/lost-reply.test.ts`)
- **QA8-E12 A value of exactly 16 KiB is sent, one byte more is not.** (`extensions/kvai/test/toolcall-arguments.test.ts`)
- **QA8-E13 The conversation still sees a large call as complete.** *Given* a `toolcall` chunk with `command: null`, *then* the live view marks the call complete and shows its title and description. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/web/live-step.test.ts`)
- **QA8-H11 A call without an id is given one.** *Given* a reply whose call has a name but an empty id, *then* the call runs, its stored id and its result's `toolCallId` are the same non-empty generated id, and the next model request carries that id in both the call and its result. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/broken-call.test.ts`)
- **QA8-E15 A call without a name is dropped, and the reply is lost.** *Given* a reply whose only call has an empty id and name, *then* the stored reply has no call, the model gets the lost-reply hint, the turn goes on, and the history stays valid for every later request. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/broken-call.test.ts`)
- **QA8-E16 A sound call next to a broken one is kept.** *Then* the reply is stored with the sound call only, and it runs. (`extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/lost-reply.test.ts`)
