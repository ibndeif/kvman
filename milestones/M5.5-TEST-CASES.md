# M5.5 — Recordings that look finished: test cases

Read: `01` §1.5; `16` the opening, §16.3, §16.5; ADR 0115, 3, 4, 8, 9, 16, 31 to 33, 35, 46, and 53 to 56.

The layers: the address check, the page's routing, the motion (the pointer's path, the typing pace, the scroll), the narration's cues, the readiness wait, the sidecar, and the input schema as unit tests; `record`'s checks before a browser starts through the test kernel; and `record` end to end with the installed Chrome against local test servers (a second local server stands in for the internet, since no test reaches the network), with the stand-in ffmpeg that counts JPEG frames and reads the first one's size, and once with the ffmpeg on the PATH.

## Happy path

### M5.5-H1 — any website's address passes the check
- **Given** no kvman port, and kvman's port 3737
- **When** `https://example.com/`, `http://192.168.1.5:3000/a`, `http://localhost:5173`, and `https://[::1]/` are checked
- **Then** each passes
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/address.test.ts`

### M5.5-H2 — a website is recorded at 2× density, and every action has its times and box
- **Given** the local test app
- **When** `record` runs at 640 × 360 with `click`, `type`, `hover`, `press`, `scroll`, and `wait`
- **Then** ffmpeg gets frames of 1280 × 720 and its arguments scale to 1280 × 720 with `-crf 14`; each answered step has `atMs` and `endMs` (`endMs` ≥ `atMs`); `click`, `type`, and `hover` also have `actMs` between them and a `box` inside the 640 × 360 viewport, and the others have neither
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/record.test.ts`

### M5.5-H3 — the default sizes
- **Given** the local test app
- **When** `record` runs with no size, then with `device: 'phone'` and no size
- **Then** the frames are 2560 × 1440, then 780 × 1688
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/record.test.ts`

### M5.5-H4 — a page in the workspace is recorded with what is beside it
- **Given** `videos/t/title.html` with a CSS animation, a stylesheet `style.css` and an image `img/pic.png` beside it
- **When** `record` runs with `page: 'videos/t/title.html'` and a `wait` of 1000 ms, and the same page is opened in a recording's context
- **Then** more than one distinct frame reached ffmpeg, and the sidecar's `kind` is `page` with `source` the page's path; in the context, the stylesheet is applied and the image loaded. (The internet can't be reached by a test; a page loading a web font from it is part of the by-hand check of M5.11.)
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/record-page.test.ts`

### M5.5-H5 — the page's routing answers files under the page's folder with their types
- **Given** a page's folder with `a.css`, `img/b.png`, `font.woff2`, and `data.json`
- **When** the paths are resolved
- **Then** each is the file under the folder, with `text/css`, `image/png`, `font/woff2`, and `application/json`; an unknown extension is `application/octet-stream`; an encoded path (`img%2Fb.png`, `a%20b.css`) is decoded
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/page-route.test.ts`

### M5.5-H6 — the pointer is hidden until its first move, then an arrow that presses and rings
- **Given** the cursor script in a page
- **When** nothing has moved, then the mouse moves, then it is pressed
- **Then** no pointer is visible at first; after the move an arrow is at the mouse; a press shrinks it to 85% and adds a ring, and the arrow returns to full size after 120 ms
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/record-page.test.ts`

### M5.5-H7 — the pointer's path: its length by distance, a gentle curve, and easing
- **Given** moves of 0, 100, 500, and 2000 px
- **When** their paths are planned
- **Then** they last 400, 400, 600, and 900 ms; each starts at its start and ends at its target; the middle point of a long move is off the straight line by less than 10% of its length; the first and last moves are shorter than the middle ones
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/motion.test.ts`

### M5.5-H8 — typing has a varying pace that is the same each time
- **Given** the text `Hello, world`
- **When** its delays are planned twice
- **Then** both plans are equal; each delay is 35 to 110 ms, plus 150 ms after a space or a punctuation mark; not all delays are equal; another text gives another plan
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/motion.test.ts`

### M5.5-H9 — scrolling is smooth and eased
- **Given** scrolls of 100, 1000, −1000, and 20 000 px
- **When** they are planned
- **Then** they last 450, 900, 900, and 3000 ms; their moves add up to the pixels exactly; the first and last moves are smaller than the middle ones
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/motion.test.ts`

### M5.5-H10 — a phone recording taps and shows the touch mark
- **Given** the local test app and `device: 'phone'`
- **When** `record` clicks Save and types a name
- **Then** the page got touch events and saved the name, and the touch mark appeared at the tap
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/record-page.test.ts`

### M5.5-H11 — steps start at their caption line
- **Given** `talk.mp3` with a sidecar of 4000 ms and three caption lines starting at 0, 1500, and 3000 ms
- **When** `record` runs with `narration: 'talk.mp3'` and a `click` with `atLine: 2`, then a `hover` with `atLine: 3`
- **Then** the click starts at 1800 ms or later and the hover at 3300 ms or later, and `durationMs` is at least 4600
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/record-page.test.ts`

### M5.5-H12 — the cue times of a narration
- **Given** captions starting at 0, 1500, and 3000 ms in a narration of 4000 ms
- **When** the cues are read
- **Then** line 2 starts at 1800 ms from the recording's start, the least length is 4600 ms, and a step whose line has passed starts at once
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/narration-cues.test.ts`

### M5.5-H13 — the sidecar is written beside the recording and read back
- **Given** a recording of the local test app to `videos/a.mp4`
- **When** `videos/a.mp4.kvvideo.json` is read
- **Then** it has `kind: 'site'`, the `source`, the width and height, `device`, `scale: 2`, `durationMs`, the call's steps, and the answer's steps as `actions`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/record.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/sidecar.test.ts`

### M5.5-H14 — progress for each step, as a key with its numbers
- **Given** the local test app
- **When** `record` runs with three steps, watched for progress
- **Then** it sends `{ key: 'kvvideo.progress.step', params: { n, of: 3 } }` for n = 1, 2, 3, in order, from source `@kvman/kvvideo`; the key has `en` and `ar` texts
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/record-page.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/catalog.test.ts`

### M5.5-H15 — the recording waits for fonts and an idle network, and hides scroll bars
- **Given** a page that requests a file after it loads, answered 800 ms later
- **When** the page is made ready
- **Then** readiness resolves after the stylesheet arrived; the page's scroll bars are hidden (`scrollbar-width` is `none`)
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/record-page.test.ts`

### M5.5-H16 — a real recording is twice its size
- **Given** the ffmpeg on the PATH
- **When** a page is recorded at 640 × 360 with a click and a wait of 1000 ms
- **Then** ffprobe reads H.264 of 1280 × 720 at 30 frames a second, without sound, as long as `durationMs` within 200 ms
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/real-ffmpeg.test.ts`

### M5.5-H17 — kvvideo is described as a maker of any kind of video
- **Given** kvvideo's package
- **When** its `package.json` description and README are read
- **Then** neither ties kvvideo to the person's app
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/bundled.test.ts`

## Edge cases

### M5.5-E1 — an address with credentials, another scheme, or not an address fails
- **When** `http://user:pass@example.com/`, `ftp://example.com/`, `file:///etc/hosts`, and `not an address` are checked
- **Then** each fails `VALIDATION_FAILED`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/address.test.ts`

### M5.5-E2 — kvman's own address stays refused, and its port aborted from any host
- **Given** kvman's port 3737
- **When** `http://127.0.0.1:3737/` and `http://localhost:3737/x` are checked, and a recorded website fetches kvman's port
- **Then** both fail `VALIDATION_FAILED`, and the fetch is aborted while another port is reached (M5.2-E3)
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/address.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/record.test.ts`

### M5.5-E3 — url and page together, or neither, fail before a browser starts
- **When** `record` runs with both `url` and `page`, then with neither
- **Then** each fails `VALIDATION_FAILED`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/record-input.test.ts`

### M5.5-E4 — a goto in a page's steps fails
- **When** `record` runs with a `page` and a `goto` step
- **Then** it fails `VALIDATION_FAILED` before a browser starts
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/record-input.test.ts`

### M5.5-E5 — a page that isn't HTML, doesn't exist, or is outside the workspace
- **When** `record` runs with `page: 'notes.txt'`, `page: 'missing.html'`, `page: '../out.html'`, and a page through a symlink out of the workspace
- **Then** they fail `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `NOT_FOUND`, `VALIDATION_FAILED`, and `VALIDATION_FAILED`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/record-input.test.ts`

### M5.5-E6 — a path out of the page's folder, or a missing file, is answered 404
- **Given** a page's folder inside a workspace, and a symlink in it to a folder outside
- **When** `../secret.txt`, `%2E%2E/secret.txt`, `link/x.txt`, and `missing.css` are resolved
- **Then** each is answered 404
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/page-route.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/record-page.test.ts`

### M5.5-E7 — atLine past the captions, atLine without a narration, a narration without a sidecar, a missing narration
- **When** `record` runs with `atLine: 4` on a narration of three lines; with `atLine` and no `narration`; with a narration that has no sidecar; with a narration that doesn't exist; with a narration that isn't audio
- **Then** they fail `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `NOT_FOUND`, and `VALIDATION_FAILED`, before a browser starts
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/record-input.test.ts`

### M5.5-E8 — atLine is not on a wait, and is a whole number from 1
- **When** the schema reads `{ do: 'wait', ms: 0, atLine: 1 }`, `{ do: 'click', target: '#a', atLine: 0 }`, and `{ do: 'click', target: '#a', atLine: 1.5 }`
- **Then** each fails, and `{ do: 'click', target: '#a', atLine: 1 }` passes
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/steps.test.ts`

### M5.5-E9 — a missing or invalid sidecar is absent
- **Given** no sidecar, one that isn't JSON, and one of another shape
- **When** each is read
- **Then** each is absent
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/sidecar.test.ts`

### M5.5-E10 — an existing sidecar is replaced only with replace
- **Given** `clip.mp4.kvvideo.json` without `clip.mp4`
- **When** `record` runs to `clip.mp4` without `replace`, then with `replace: true`
- **Then** the first fails `VALIDATION_FAILED` before a browser starts; the second writes both files
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/record-input.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/record.test.ts`

### M5.5-E11 — readiness waits at most 10 s for an idle network
- **Given** a page whose network never goes idle, and a page whose readiness fails for another reason
- **When** readiness runs
- **Then** the first goes on after the idle wait times out; the second's error is thrown
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/page-ready.test.ts`

### M5.5-E12 — the phone's size and the desktop's limits
- **When** the schema reads `device: 'tablet'`, and `device: 'phone'` with `width: 412` and `height: 916`
- **Then** the first fails, the second passes
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/steps.test.ts`
