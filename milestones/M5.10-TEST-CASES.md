# M5.10 — Recording your own voice: test cases

Read: `08` §8.5 (`ask record`), §8.7 (the record card); `16` §16.7; `14` §14.6, §14.10; `15` §15.4; ADR 0115, 24 to 29, 49 to 51, and 64.

The layers: the card's pure parts (the line's runs, lengths, file types and names) and the take's cleaning arguments as unit tests; `ask record` through the test kernel with the fake model and uploads made as files of the workspace; the card in `happy-dom` with a stand-in microphone, recorder, and upload; `voice` through the test kernel with stand-in programs and once with the ffmpeg on the PATH; the desktop shell's permission rule in Rust; and the VS Code frame's `allow`.

## Happy path

### M5.10-H1 — the agent asks for a recording and gets the takes in the workspace
- **Given** a line with an example `.mp3` in the workspace, and a line without
- **When** the agent calls `ask record`, and the person answers with two uploaded takes (WebM and MP4)
- **Then** the pending question has its kind, its lines, and `exampleFileIds` (an id, then `null`); the takes are written as `<folder>/line-1.webm` and `line-2.m4a`, the uploads are deleted, and the model reads `{"takes":[{"file":"<folder>/line-1.webm","durationMs":…},…]}` then the answered note
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/ask-record.test.ts`

### M5.10-H2 — an identical retry applies nothing again; a skip is skipped
- **When** the same answer is sent again after it applied, and another question is dismissed
- **Then** the retry is acknowledged with no new file work; the dismissed question's result is the skipped note
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/ask-record.test.ts`

### M5.10-H3 — the card guides the person through each line
- **Given** a record question with a tone, a pace, a line with stressed words, pauses, a target of 5 s, and an example
- **When** it is shown
- **Then** the stressed words are bold (in any case), a pause mark follows each pause word, the tone and pace and "about 5 s" show, "Hear an example" plays the example's address, and "Use these takes" is off
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/web/record-card.test.ts`

### M5.10-H4 — recording a take: a countdown, the time, Stop, then Play and Again
- **Given** a stand-in microphone and recorder
- **When** Record is pressed, 3 s pass, then Stop
- **Then** the countdown shows 3, 2, 1; then "Recording 0:01" with Stop; then the take's length with Play and Again; with a take for every line, "Use these takes" uploads each as `line-<n>.<ext>` with its type and answers `{ takes: [{ fileId, durationMs }] }`
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/web/record-card.test.ts`

### M5.10-H5 — the answered card lists each line with its take's length
- **Given** an answered recording of two lines
- **When** it is shown
- **Then** each line's text shows with its length as `m:ss`
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/web/record-card.test.ts`

### M5.10-H6 — the desktop app grants the microphone to kvman's page only
- **When** the shell decides a microphone request on kvman's origin, on another origin, and before kvman runs
- **Then** it allows the first and denies the others
- Test: `packages/desktop/src-tauri/tests/microphone.rs`

### M5.10-H7 — VS Code's frame may ask for the microphone
- **Then** the frame's `allow` is `clipboard-read; clipboard-write; microphone`
- Test: `packages/vscode/test/web/view-html.test.ts`

### M5.10-H8 — `voice` cleans each take and joins them with exact take starts
- **Given** two takes and their texts, with stand-in programs
- **When** `voice` runs, then again with `clean: false`
- **Then** each take was cleaned with the four filters (and only converted the second time), the takes were joined with 250 ms between them, each take's captions start where it does, and the sidecar has the captions; the takes are unchanged
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/voice.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/voice-args.test.ts`

### M5.10-H9 — a real voice: silence trimmed, level evened
- **Given** the ffmpeg on the PATH, and two takes of a tone with a second of silence before and after
- **When** `voice` runs
- **Then** the narration is shorter than the takes together (the silences trimmed to 150 ms), and each caption starts within 100 ms of its take
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/real-voice.test.ts`

### M5.10-H10 — the card's parts
- **When** a line is split, a length shown, a recorder's type chosen, and a take named
- **Then** the runs keep the text with the stressed and paused words marked; `0:05`; WebM with Opus first, then MP4; `line-2.m4a` for `audio/mp4`; `audio/webm;codecs=opus` is taken as `audio/webm`
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/web/record-parts.test.ts`

## Edge cases

### M5.10-E1 — a record question that can't be asked
- **When** a stress word isn't in its line; an example is a `.txt`, over 10 MiB, or missing; the folder is outside the workspace; there are 21 lines
- **Then** the call fails `VALIDATION_FAILED` (`NOT_FOUND` for the missing example), and no question is pending
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/ask-record.test.ts`

### M5.10-E2 — takes that can't be used
- **When** the answer has one take for two lines, a take that is a PNG, one over 25 MiB, or an id that isn't a file
- **Then** it fails `VALIDATION_FAILED`, the question stays pending, and nothing is written
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/ask-record.test.ts`

### M5.10-E3 — no microphone, or a refused one
- **When** the browser has no `mediaDevices`, and when `getUserMedia` is refused
- **Then** the card says "The microphone can't be opened here." or "Microphone access was refused…", offers "Open this chat in a browser", and Upload a file stays on each line
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/web/record-card.test.ts`

### M5.10-E4 — an uploaded file that isn't sound
- **When** the person chooses a `.png` with Upload a file
- **Then** a toast says it isn't a sound file kvman takes, and the line has no take
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/web/record-card.test.ts`

### M5.10-E5 — `voice`'s inputs
- **When** a take isn't sound, doesn't exist, or is outside the workspace; 21 lines; an output that exists without `replace`
- **Then** `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `NOT_FOUND`, `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `VALIDATION_FAILED`, before ffmpeg runs; a failing ffmpeg fails `kvvideo/RENDER_FAILED` with its take
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/voice.test.ts`

### M5.10-E6 — the camera, the screen, and other devices are refused
- **Then** the shell denies the camera, screen capture, MIDI, and sensors on every page, and leaves the clipboard and notifications to the engine
- Test: `packages/desktop/src-tauri/tests/microphone.rs`
