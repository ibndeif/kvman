# M5.8 — The final pass: transitions, sound, and what's beside the video: test cases

Read: `16` §16.8 (transitions, sound effects, the render, beside the video, other sizes, the answer, the player's copy), §16.9; ADR 0115, 2, 12, 14, 19, 22, 37, 38, 41, 44, 45, and 62.

The layers: the scenes' lengths and starts, the transitions, the sound effects' times, the parts' and the final pass's arguments, the loudness measure's reading, and the `.srt` as unit tests; `compose` through the test kernel with stand-in ffmpeg and ffprobe programs that log each run and write what the next one reads; the card in `happy-dom`; and real renders with the ffmpeg on the PATH (and Chrome for a page's other size), whose sound and pictures are measured.

## Happy path

### M5.8-H1 — a scene's length and its narration's place
- **When** scenes are planned with a narration of 2000 ms and no `durationMs`, with `durationMs`, and with neither (a video part of 4000 ms)
- **Then** their lengths are 2600, the `durationMs`, and 4000; a part's sound places the narration at 300 ms, fades 20 ms at both ends, and is PCM at 48 kHz, stereo, in a `.mkv` encoded `-preset veryfast -crf 14`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-plan.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-args.test.ts`

### M5.8-H2 — the transitions and the scenes' starts
- **Given** three scenes of 3000, 2000, and 4000 ms, the first with no transition, the second with `slide` toward `start` for 400 ms
- **When** they are planned in an `ltr` and an `rtl` video
- **Then** the first is a crossfade of 500 ms, the second `slideleft` (ltr) or `slideright` (rtl) of 400 ms; the scenes start at 0, 2500, and 4100; the video is 8100 ms long; `toward: 'end'`, `up`, and `down` give `slideright`, `slideup`, and `slidedown` in `ltr`; `fade-black` gives `fadeblack`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/transitions.test.ts`

### M5.8-H3 — a default crossfade fits a short scene
- **When** a scene of 600 ms with no transition is followed by one of 3000 ms
- **Then** its crossfade is 300 ms
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/transitions.test.ts`

### M5.8-H4 — the final pass joins with xfade and acrossfade, and a cut with concat
- **Given** three parts: a crossfade of 500 ms, then a cut
- **When** the final pass's graph is made
- **Then** the video is `xfade=transition=fade:duration=0.500:offset=2.500` then `concat`, and the sound `acrossfade=d=0.500` then `concat`, each in order
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/final-pass.test.ts`

### M5.8-H5 — the sound effects at their times
- **Given** a recorded website's scene starting at 2500 ms in the video, its part from 1000 ms, with a click acting at 1800 ms, a type acting at 2200 and ending at 3200 ms, a callout from 500 ms, and a slide into the next scene
- **When** the sound effects are found
- **Then** a click at 3300, key taps from 3820 to 4700, a pop at 3000, and a whoosh at the slide's start; `sounds: false` on the scene or the video removes them
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/sound-effects.test.ts`

### M5.8-H6 — the sound effects in the final mix
- **When** the final pass's graph has a click, key taps, and a pop
- **Then** each sound file is read once (the key taps looped), each event is delayed to its time, the click and pop 18 dB down and the taps 24 dB down, and all are mixed with the joined sound without normalizing
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/final-pass.test.ts`

### M5.8-H7 — loudness in two passes
- **Given** a first pass whose output ends with loudnorm's JSON
- **When** it is read and the second pass's filter made
- **Then** the second pass has `loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1:LRA=11` with the measured `I`, `TP`, `LRA`, threshold, and offset, and `linear=true`, then `aresample=48000`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/final-pass.test.ts`

### M5.8-H8 — compose makes parts, measures, then encodes, and writes what is beside the video
- **Given** two scenes with captions and a narration
- **When** `compose` runs with the stand-in programs
- **Then** ffmpeg ran for each part (`part-1.mkv`, `part-2.mkv`), the measure, the final encode (`libx264 -preset medium -crf 18`, AAC 192k, `+faststart`), and the poster; the video, `<name>.srt`, `<name>.jpg`, and the sidecar are beside the file; the answer has `poster` and `durationMs` less the crossfade; progress gave `kvvideo.progress.scene` for each part and `kvvideo.progress.final`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose-final.test.ts`

### M5.8-H9 — the `.srt` is on the video's time line
- **Given** two scenes, the second starting at 2500 ms, each with a narration and a caption at 0 ms
- **When** the `.srt` is made
- **Then** its cues are at 0:00:00,300 and 0:00:02,800, numbered from 1, with `-->` and a blank line between them
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/srt.test.ts`

### M5.8-H10 — other sizes, framed again
- **Given** a recorded website's scene and an extra size of 1080 × 1920
- **When** `compose` runs
- **Then** the extra video is `<name>-1080x1920.mp4` with its own `.srt` and poster, listed in `others` with its bytes; its parts were made at 1080 × 1920
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose-final.test.ts`

### M5.8-H11 — a recorded page is recorded again at another shape
- **Given** a page recorded at 1280 × 720, and an extra size of 1080 × 1920
- **When** `compose` runs with Chrome and the ffmpeg on the PATH
- **Then** the extra video's page scene was recorded again at 720 × 1280 (its frames are 1440 × 2560 before they are fitted)
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/real-final.test.ts`

### M5.8-H12 — the poster's copy and the card
- **Given** a composed video
- **When** its card is shown
- **Then** the player's copy has a poster copy (`image/jpeg`); the card's `<video>` has the poster's address and lists the other sizes' paths
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose-final.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/web/player.test.ts`

### M5.8-H13 — a real final pass: transitions, effects, and loudness
- **Given** the ffmpeg on the PATH, two image scenes with a tone narration and a slide between them
- **When** they are composed
- **Then** the video is as long as the answer says within 100 ms, its loudness is −14 ± 1 LUFS with its true peak under −1 dBTP, and the frame in the middle of the slide is neither scene alone
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/real-final.test.ts`

## Edge cases

### M5.8-E1 — a transition too long, or on the last scene
- **When** a transition of 1600 ms joins scenes of 3000 and 3000 ms; one of 400 ms a scene of 700 ms; the last scene has a transition; `ms` is 2001
- **Then** each fails `VALIDATION_FAILED` before ffmpeg runs
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/transitions.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose-final.test.ts`

### M5.8-E2 — extra sizes that repeat
- **When** `extraSizes` has the main size, the same size twice, or three sizes
- **Then** each fails `VALIDATION_FAILED`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose-final.test.ts`

### M5.8-E3 — a failure in the final pass, or in the measure
- **When** the measure exits 1, or the final encode exits 1
- **Then** `compose` fails `kvvideo/RENDER_FAILED` with `scene: null` and the last lines; nothing is written beside the file
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose-final.test.ts`

### M5.8-E4 — loudnorm's answer without its JSON
- **When** the measure's output has no JSON object, or one without `input_i`
- **Then** it fails `kvvideo/RENDER_FAILED` with `scene: null`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/final-pass.test.ts`

### M5.8-E5 — captions off, and one scene
- **When** one scene is composed with captions off
- **Then** no `.srt` is written and the final pass has no `xfade`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose-final.test.ts`

### M5.8-E6 — the timeout
- **Given** kvvideo's registrations
- **When** `compose`'s timeout is read
- **Then** it is 60 minutes
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose-final.test.ts`
