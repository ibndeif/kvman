# M5.14 — kvvideo's address in a page: the motion kit, the brand, the fonts, the libraries, and failed loads: test cases

Read: `16` §16.5 (A page, kvvideo's address in a page, Failed loads, The motion kit, Looking); ADR 0120.

The layers: the address's answers and `brand.css` as units; `record` and `look` of pages that use the address, and their failed loads, end to end with the installed Chrome; the motion kit's classes by `look` at chosen moments, end to end.

## Happy path

### M5.14-H1 — `kvvideo/brand.css` gives the brand's colours and font
- **Given** a workspace brand `{ logo: 'brand/logo.png', colors: { primary: '#047857', background: '#F8FAFC', text: '#0F172A' }, font: 'Amiri' }`
- **When** `brand.css` is made
- **Then** its `:root` has `--brand-primary: #047857`, `--brand-background: #F8FAFC`, `--brand-text: #0F172A`, and `--brand-font: 'Amiri'`, and it has an `@font-face` for each bundled font at `fonts/<file>`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-address.test.ts`

### M5.14-H2 — a brand font file of the workspace is declared by its family
- **Given** a brand whose `font` is `brand/Brand-Bold.ttf`, whose family is "Lato"
- **When** `brand.css` is made and `kvvideo/fonts/brand.ttf` is asked
- **Then** `--brand-font` is `'Lato'`, an `@font-face` names "Lato" at `fonts/brand.ttf`, and that address answers the file
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-address.test.ts`

### M5.14-H3 — with no brand, the defaults, by the page's language
- **Given** no brand
- **When** `brand.css` is made
- **Then** the colours are `#2563EB`, `#0F172A`, `#F8FAFC`, `--brand-font` is `'Poppins'`, and `:root:lang(ar)` sets it to `'IBM Plex Sans Arabic'`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-address.test.ts`

### M5.14-H4 — the address answers the kit, the logo, the fonts, and the libraries
- **Given** a brand with a `.png` logo
- **When** `kvvideo/motion.css`, `kvvideo/logo.png`, `kvvideo/fonts/Amiri-Bold.ttf`, `kvvideo/lib/three/build/three.module.js`, `kvvideo/lib/three/examples/jsm/geometries/RoundedBoxGeometry.js`, `kvvideo/lib/anime.js`, and `kvvideo/lib/lottie.js` are asked
- **Then** each is answered with its file and its content type
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-address.test.ts`

### M5.14-H5 — a page is drawn in the brand's font and colours, with its logo
- **Given** a branded workspace whose logo is a green square, and a page that links `kvvideo/brand.css` and shows `kvvideo/logo.png` on `var(--brand-background)`
- **When** `look` shoots it
- **Then** the background is the brand's, the logo is drawn, `document.fonts.check` finds the brand's font, and `failed` is empty
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/scene-address.test.ts`

### M5.14-H6 — three.js and its addons, anime.js, and lottie-web load
- **Given** a page with an import map of `three` to `kvvideo/lib/three/build/three.module.js` and `three/addons/` to `kvvideo/lib/three/examples/jsm/`, drawing a red cube with an addon geometry, and importing `kvvideo/lib/anime.js` and loading `kvvideo/lib/lottie.js`
- **When** `look` shoots it
- **Then** the cube is drawn red in the middle, the page saw anime.js's `animate` and lottie-web's `loadAnimation`, and `failed` is empty
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/scene-address.test.ts`

### M5.14-H7 — a file the page can't load is reported, in `look` and `record`
- **Given** a page whose stylesheet names a font at `../outside/font.woff2`
- **When** `look` and `record` take it
- **Then** each answers `failed: [{ url: 'http://page.kvvideo.invalid/outside/font.woff2', status: 404 }]`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/scene-address.test.ts`

### M5.14-H8 — the motion kit's entrances, emphasis, ambient motion, and exit
- **Given** a page with boxes of `kv-rise` at `--at: 1s`, `kv-slide`, `kv-wipe`, `kv-pop`, `kv-words` with three children, `kv-draw`, `kv-count` from 0 to 100, `kv-float` with `kv-rise`, `kv-pulse` at `--em-at: 2s`, and `kv-fade kv-out` at `--out: 3s`
- **When** its clock is set to chosen moments and each box's computed style is read
- **Then** each box is where its class says: hidden before its start, moving in, in place after; the slide comes from the left; the wipe shows its left side first; the third word is still hidden when the first shows; the line is part drawn half way and drawn after; the count is between 0 and 100 while it runs and 100 after; the floating box both rose and floats; the pulsed box is larger at 2350 ms; the leaving box is there at 2000 ms and gone at 3600 ms
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/motion-kit.test.ts`

### M5.14-H9 — the kit moves along the line of a right-to-left page
- **Given** the same `kv-slide` and `kv-wipe` boxes in a `dir="rtl"` page
- **When** their computed styles are read part way in
- **Then** the slide comes from the right and the wipe shows its right side first
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/motion-kit.test.ts`

## Edge cases

### M5.14-E1 — the address is kvvideo's, never the page's folder
- **Given** a page folder with its own `kvvideo/motion.css`
- **When** `kvvideo/motion.css` is asked
- **Then** kvvideo's kit is answered, not the folder's file
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-address.test.ts`

### M5.14-E2 — anything else under the address is 404
- **Given** a brand without a logo, one with a `.svg` logo, and one whose logo and font files were removed
- **When** `kvvideo/logo.png`, `kvvideo/other.css`, `kvvideo/fonts/missing.ttf`, `kvvideo/fonts/brand.ttf` with no brand font, `kvvideo/fonts/../../package.json`, `kvvideo/lib/three/src/Three.js`, `kvvideo/lib/three/package.json`, `kvvideo/lib/three/build/../../package.json`, and `kvvideo/lib/three/build` are asked
- **Then** each is answered 404, the `.svg` logo is served as `logo.svg`, and the removed files are left out
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-address.test.ts`

### M5.14-E3 — failed loads are listed once each, at most 20, and kvman's own address is left out
- **Given** the list of failed loads for kvman's port 3737
- **When** the same missing file is added twice, a request to `127.0.0.1:3737`, then 25 other missing files
- **Then** it has 20 entries, the repeated file once, and none on kvman's port
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-address.test.ts`

### M5.14-E4 — a website's request that fails is listed with status 0
- **Given** a local website whose page asks an address that refuses connections
- **When** `look` takes it
- **Then** `failed` lists it with status 0
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/scene-address.test.ts`
