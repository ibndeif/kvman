# M5.15 — Scenes drawn frame by frame at 60 frames a second, kvvideo's clock, sound cues, the page's push-in, and branded captions: test cases

Read: `16` §16.3 (Progress, The sidecar), §16.5 (Drawn frame by frame, kvvideo's clock, Sound cues, Looking), §16.8 (The picture, A page's camera, Captions, Sound effects, The render); ADR 0120.

The layers: the drawn length, the camera's push-in, the sound events, the caption style, the scene and final arguments, and the sidecar as units; drawing a page, its clock, its cues, and `look` on the clock end to end with the installed Chrome and the real ffmpeg; a composed video's frame rate end to end.

## Happy path

### M5.15-H1 — a page of only waits is drawn frame by frame at 60 frames a second
- **Given** a page whose box slides with a 2 s linear CSS animation, recorded with `steps: [{ do: 'wait', ms: 1000 }]`
- **When** it is recorded with the real ffmpeg
- **Then** the file is 60 frames a second, 150 frames long (500 + 1000 + 1000 ms), `durationMs` 2500, every frame of the first 2 s distinct from the one before, and its sidecar's `drawn` is `true`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/drawn-page.test.ts`

### M5.15-H2 — a drawn page is the same at every recording
- **Given** the same page
- **When** it is recorded twice
- **Then** the two files' frames are the same, frame for frame
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/drawn-page.test.ts`

### M5.15-H3 — scripts run on kvvideo's clock, alike in `look` and `record`
- **Given** a page that moves a box by `requestAnimationFrame` (x = 0.3 px per ms of `performance.now()`), turns it from white to grey at 500 ms with `setTimeout`, and sizes it by `Math.random()`
- **When** `look` shoots it at 1000 ms and `record` draws it
- **Then** the box is at 300 px, grey, and the same width in the look and in the recording's frame at 1000 ms
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/drawn-page.test.ts`

### M5.15-H4 — a three.js scene turns on kvvideo's clock
- **Given** a page whose three.js cube turns by `requestAnimationFrame` with the clock's time
- **When** `look` shoots it at 0 and 1000 ms twice
- **Then** the two calls give the same frames, and the frames at 0 and 1000 ms differ
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/drawn-page.test.ts`

### M5.15-H5 — a sound cue is answered at its element's start
- **Given** a drawn page whose element with `data-sound="pop"` enters with an animation delayed 1.2 s
- **When** it is recorded
- **Then** `sounds` is `[{ sound: 'pop', atMs }]` with `atMs` within one frame of 1200, and the sidecar holds the same
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/drawn-page.test.ts`

### M5.15-H6 — a drawn page's cues become sound effects; a crossfade has a soft whoosh
- **Given** a scene of a drawn page with a pop cue at 1200 ms and a click cue at 9000 ms past its part, then a crossfade into the next scene
- **When** the video's sound events are made
- **Then** there is a pop at the scene's start plus 1200 ms, no click, and a soft whoosh at the next scene's start; the final pass mixes the soft whoosh 26 dB down
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/sound-effects.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/final-pass.test.ts`

### M5.15-H7 — a recorded page's `auto` camera pushes in
- **Given** a scene of a recorded page, 4000 ms long, from 500 ms of its part
- **When** its look is made
- **Then** its camera is one even shot from 1× to 1.05×, centred, from 500 to 4500 ms of the recording, at the recording's scale
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-plan.test.ts`

### M5.15-H8 — the video is 60 frames a second
- **Given** a scene
- **When** its part's and the final pass's arguments are made
- **Then** the clip is brought to 60 frames a second, stills loop at 60, the camera reads time as frames ÷ 60, and both encode with `-r 60`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/scene-args.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/final-pass.test.ts`, `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/camera.test.ts`

### M5.15-H9 — a composed video with a website recording is 60 frames a second
- **Given** a website recorded in real time at 30 frames a second
- **When** it is composed with the real ffmpeg
- **Then** the video's frame rate is 60
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/real-final.test.ts`

### M5.15-H10 — the captions take the brand by default
- **Given** a brand whose `primary` is `#047857`
- **When** captions are styled with no `style`
- **Then** the look is `boxed`, the box `#047857` at 90% opacity (`&H1A577804`), and the text white
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/ass-file.test.ts`

### M5.15-H11 — with no brand, the captions are outlined
- **Given** no brand
- **When** captions are styled with no `style`, and with `boxed`
- **Then** the first is `outline`, and the boxed one is white on black at 65%
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/ass-file.test.ts`

### M5.15-H12 — drawing reports its frames
- **Given** a drawn page of 150 frames
- **When** it is recorded
- **Then** progress chunks `kvvideo.progress.frame` come every 30 frames, `{ n: 30, of: 150 }` to `{ n: 150, of: 150 }`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/drawn-page.test.ts`

### M5.15-H13 — a recording's sidecar says how it was made
- **Given** a recording's sidecar with `drawn` and `sounds`, and one written before them
- **When** each is read
- **Then** the first keeps them, and the second reads as `drawn: false`, `sounds: []`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/sidecar.test.ts`

## Edge cases

### M5.15-E1 — a page with clicks is recorded in real time
- **Given** a page recorded with a `click` step
- **When** it is recorded
- **Then** its sidecar's `drawn` is `false`, its file 30 frames a second, and `sounds` empty
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/drawn-page.test.ts`

### M5.15-E2 — a drawn page lasts as long as its narration asks
- **Given** a page of no steps and a narration of 3000 ms
- **When** its length is planned
- **Then** it is 3600 ms (300 + 3000 + 300), and with no narration 1500 ms; with waits of 1000 and 500 ms it is 3000 ms
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/drawn-length.test.ts`

### M5.15-E3 — timers keep their order, intervals repeat, cleared ones don't run, and a failing callback doesn't stop the rest
- **Given** a page with an interval of 200 ms that counts, a timeout at 300 ms that is cleared, a timeout at 100 ms that throws, and one at 150 ms that marks the page
- **When** `look` shoots it at 1000 ms
- **Then** the count is 5, the cleared timeout never ran, and the page is marked
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/drawn-page.test.ts`

### M5.15-E4 — an unknown sound, and an element cued once
- **Given** a drawn page with `data-sound="boom"` on an entering element, and `data-sound="click"` on an element with a looping animation
- **When** it is recorded
- **Then** `sounds` has only the click, once
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/drawn-page.test.ts`

### M5.15-E5 — a light brand gets near-black caption text
- **Given** a brand whose `primary` is `#FFD54A`
- **When** captions are boxed
- **Then** the text is `#111111`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/ass-file.test.ts`
