# M5.12 — A production workflow, `video look`, the scene's clock, and no `.srt`: test cases

Read: `16` §16.1, §16.3, §16.5 (The page's clock, Looking), §16.8 (Beside the video, Other sizes), §16.12; `08` §8.2 (Deliberate); ADR 0117.

The layers: the guide, the connector, and `look`'s input through the test kernel; the lead's method as a unit; the page's clock and `look`'s frames end to end with the installed Chrome; `compose` with the stand-in ffmpeg.

## Happy path

### M5.12-H1 — after `/video`, the prompt holds the ten stages, then the production rules, then the rules
- **Given** a chat after `/video`
- **When** its prompt is read
- **Then** its "Making videos" section has, in order, Ready, Brief, Research, Concept, Treatment, Production map, Voice first, Pictures, Assemble and review, Deliver, then Story, Look, Motion, Text, Sound, Accuracy and licences, then Rules; it names `brief.md`, `style.css`, `video look`, the worker that debates approaches, `ask choice`, the 2.5 and 2 words a second, `animation-delay`, the motion graphics designer, and at most three rounds, and has no `.srt`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/registration.test.ts`

### M5.12-H2 — the connector has `look`, with images and an example its command takes
- **Given** kvvideo started
- **When** the `video` connector is listed
- **Then** `look` comes after `record`, runs `kvvideo.frame.capture`, is a command with `images: true`, and has one example that `look`'s input takes
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/registration.test.ts`

### M5.12-H3 — the lead deliberates when the person asks or a guide says when to
- **Given** the lead's engineering method, asking and deciding
- **When** its Deliberate part is read
- **Then** it starts "Only when the person asks for the approaches to be debated, or a guide in your prompt says when to:", and still names no worker
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/prompt-method.test.ts`

### M5.12-H4 — a page's animation starts with the recording, though the page waited first
- **Given** a page whose box moves with a 1 s linear animation from its load, recorded after the 500 ms of network quiet `record` waits for
- **When** it is recorded with the real ffmpeg
- **Then** the recording's first frame shows the box at its start, and a frame at 0.5 s shows it about half way
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/page-clock.test.ts`

### M5.12-H5 — `look` answers one JPEG per moment, at the CSS size, in increasing order
- **Given** an animated page of the workspace
- **When** `look` is called with `atMs: [2000, 0, 1000]` and a 640 × 360 viewport
- **Then** it answers three images named `look-1.jpg` to `look-3.jpg` with `atMs` 0, 1000, and 2000, each a JPEG of 640 × 360 in the kernel's files, the box drawn where its animation is at that moment, and writes nothing in the workspace
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/look.test.ts`

### M5.12-H6 — `look` at 1 s when no moment is given, and a website as well as a page
- **Given** a local website
- **When** `look` is called with its `url` and no `atMs`, on a phone
- **Then** it answers one image at `atMs` 1000, of 390 × 844
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/look.test.ts`

### M5.12-H7 — two calls give the same frames
- **Given** an animated page
- **When** `look` is called twice with the same moments
- **Then** both calls' images have the same bytes
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/look.test.ts`

### M5.12-H8 — `compose` writes no `.srt`, for the video or its other sizes
- **Given** two narrated scenes with captions and an extra size
- **When** they are composed
- **Then** the video, its poster, its sidecar, and the extra size and its poster are written, and no `.srt` beside either
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose-final.test.ts`

### M5.12-H9 — nothing of kvvideo is in a chat's prompt before `/video` (ADR 0117, 21)
- **Given** a chat where `/video` hasn't run, beside one where it has
- **When** both prompts are read
- **Then** the first has no "Making videos" section, no `video` connector, and none of `brief.md`, `style.css`, `video look`, or "motion graphics designer"; the second has them all
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/registration.test.ts`

## Edge cases

### M5.12-E1 — moments out of their limits fail before a browser starts
- **When** `look` is called with 7 moments, an empty list, a repeated moment, a moment of −1 or 60 001, or a fraction
- **Then** each fails `VALIDATION_FAILED`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/look-input.test.ts`

### M5.12-E2 — a source like `record`'s
- **When** `look` is called with both `url` and `page`, neither, a page that isn't `.html` or is outside the workspace, a page that doesn't exist, an address that isn't http or https, kvman's own address, or an odd width
- **Then** each fails `VALIDATION_FAILED`, the missing page `NOT_FOUND`, before a browser starts
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/look-input.test.ts`

### M5.12-E3 — a page that doesn't load
- **Given** an address whose port nothing listens on
- **When** `look` opens it
- **Then** it fails `kvvideo/STEP_FAILED` with `step` 0 and a reason
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/look.test.ts`

### M5.12-E4 — no browser
- **Given** no channel of `kvvideo.browser` starts
- **Then** `look` fails `kvvideo/BROWSER_MISSING` with the channels tried, through the same start as `record`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/browser-start.test.ts` (M5.2-E8, the one start both commands use)

### M5.12-E5 — past 24 frames, the oldest are deleted with their files
- **Given** five `look` calls of 6 moments each
- **Then** `lookFrames` has 24 rows, the six frames of the first call are no longer in the kernel's files, and the newest are
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/look.test.ts`

### M5.12-E6 — `look` has 2 minutes, is public, and isn't retried
- **When** its registration is read
- **Then** its timeout is 120 000 ms, it is public, and its retries are 0
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/look-input.test.ts`

### M5.12-E7 — captions off: still no `.srt` (M5.8-E5)
- **When** one scene is composed with captions off
- **Then** no `.srt` is written
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/compose-final.test.ts`
