# M5.13 — Fixes from the KVCare promotion: loudness, review's reasons, even captions, phrases to stress, and the narration set up by the lead: test cases

Read: `16` §16.3, §16.4, §16.6 (Captions), §16.7, §16.8 (The render), §16.10; `08` §8.5 (`ask record`), §8.7 (the record card); ADR 0120.

The layers: the caption cutting, the final pass's arguments, and review's analysis as units; `narration-set`, `check`, and `voice` through the test kernel with stand-in programs; `ask record`'s check through kvcoder's test kernel and the card's parts as a unit; the loudness of a composed video end to end with the real ffmpeg.

## Happy path

### M5.13-H1 — a loud narration composes into a video whose true peak is at most −1 dBTP
- **Given** a scene whose narration is a tone peaking at 0 dBFS
- **When** it is composed and reviewed with the real ffmpeg
- **Then** `review`'s true peak is at or below −1 dBTP, its integrated loudness within −15 to −13 LUFS, and it reports no `loudness` problem
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/real-final.test.ts`

### M5.13-H2 — the final pass aims at −2 dBTP
- **Given** a final plan
- **When** its loudness filter is made, measuring and applying
- **Then** both say `I=-14:TP=-2:LRA=11`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/final-pass.test.ts`

### M5.13-H3 — each review problem says what was measured and its bound
- **Given** ffmpeg's output with a 3.6 s silence, a 0.8 s black picture, a 3.5 s still picture, and a loudness of −16.0 LUFS with a true peak of −0.7 dBTP
- **When** it is analysed
- **Then** the problems' reasons are "No sound for 3.6 s.", "A black picture for 0.8 s.", "The picture doesn't change for 3.5 s.", and "The integrated loudness is -16.0 LUFS, outside -15 to -13 LUFS. The true peak is -0.7 dBTP, above -1 dBTP."
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/review-analysis.test.ts`

### M5.13-H4 — a still picture of 3 s or more is frozen
- **Given** a video
- **When** it is analysed
- **Then** ffmpeg's `freezedetect` is asked for 3 s (`d=3`)
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/review-analysis.test.ts`

### M5.13-H5 — an end card's short silence isn't a problem
- **Given** a 29.2 s video whose sound is silent from 25.4 s to its end
- **When** it is analysed
- **Then** there is no `silence` problem
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/review-analysis.test.ts`

### M5.13-H6 — a long sentence is cut into even lines
- **Given** the sentence "والفريق الطبي يقدر يوثّق بيانات الزيارة في السجل الطبي للمريض." (62 characters)
- **When** it is cut into caption lines
- **Then** it has two lines, neither over 42 characters, differing in length by at most the length of one word, and the first doesn't end with "في"
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/captions.test.ts`

### M5.13-H7 — a break after punctuation is preferred
- **Given** "ومن تسجيل المدفوعات للملخصات المالية، تقدر تتابع جزء مهم من شغل العيادة."
- **When** it is cut into caption lines
- **Then** the first line ends with "المالية،"
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/captions.test.ts`

### M5.13-H8 — `voice` captions each sentence of a take on its own
- **Given** a take of "اكتشف كي في كير بنفسك. اطلب عرضًا توضيحيًا لعيادتك."
- **When** `voice` makes the narration
- **Then** its captions are "اكتشف كي في كير بنفسك." and "اطلب عرضًا توضيحيًا لعيادتك.", times shared by characters, the second starting where the first ends
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/voice.test.ts`

### M5.13-H9 — `narration-set` sets Piper with a voice file anywhere
- **Given** a Piper voice `ar.onnx` with `ar.onnx.json` beside it, outside the workspace
- **When** `video narration-set { service: 'piper', voice: <its full path> }` runs
- **Then** it answers `{}`, and the global `kvvideo.narration.service` is `piper`, `.voice` the path, `.model` `null`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/narration-set.test.ts`

### M5.13-H10 — `narration-set` sets a service by voice id and model
- **Given** kvvideo started
- **When** `video narration-set { service: 'openai', voice: 'nova', model: 'gpt-4o-mini-tts' }` runs, then `{ service: 'elevenlabs' }`
- **Then** the settings are `openai`, `nova`, `gpt-4o-mini-tts`, then `elevenlabs`, `null`, `null`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/narration-set.test.ts`

### M5.13-H11 — `check` answers kvvideo's tools folder
- **Given** kvman's home `<home>`
- **When** `check` runs
- **Then** its `narration.toolsFolder` is `<home>/kvvideo`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/setup.test.ts`

### M5.13-H12 — the connector has `narration-set` with an example its command takes
- **Given** kvvideo started
- **When** the `video` connector is listed
- **Then** `narration-set` runs `kvvideo.narration.set`, a command, with one example that its input takes

### M5.13-H13 — `ask record` takes a run of words to stress and to pause after
- **Given** the line "والفريق الطبي يقدر يوثّق بيانات الزيارة في السجل الطبي للمريض."

### M5.13-H14 — the card bolds every word of a run and pauses after its last
- **Given** the same line, stress and pauses

## Edge cases

### M5.13-E1 — a silence before the end, or longer than 5 s at the end, is a problem
- **Given** a 4 s silence ending 2 s before the video's end, and a 6 s silence reaching its end
- **When** each is analysed
- **Then** each is a `silence` problem
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/review-analysis.test.ts`

### M5.13-E2 — only the loudness that is off is told
- **Given** −14.0 LUFS with a true peak of −0.4 dBTP, and −18.2 LUFS with −3.0 dBTP
- **When** each is analysed
- **Then** the reasons are "The true peak is -0.4 dBTP, above -1 dBTP." and "The integrated loudness is -18.2 LUFS, outside -15 to -13 LUFS."
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/review-analysis.test.ts`

### M5.13-E3 — short sentences and long words
- **Given** a sentence of 42 characters, and one with a word of 50 characters
- **When** each is cut
- **Then** the first is one line, and the long word is a line of its own
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/captions.test.ts`

### M5.13-E4 — a punctuation break that would make more or longer lines is not taken
- **Given** a 70-character sentence whose only comma is after its second word
- **When** it is cut
- **Then** it has two even lines, and the first doesn't end at the comma
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/captions.test.ts`

### M5.13-E5 — Piper needs a real voice file and no model
- **Given** kvvideo started
- **When** `narration-set` runs for Piper with no voice; a voice that isn't `.onnx`; a missing `.onnx`; an `.onnx` without its `.onnx.json`; a relative path; and a model
- **Then** each fails `VALIDATION_FAILED`, and no setting changes
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/narration-set.test.ts`

### M5.13-E6 — an unknown service fails
- **Given** kvvideo started
- **When** `narration-set { service: 'azure' }` runs
- **Then** it fails `VALIDATION_FAILED`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/narration-set.test.ts`

### M5.13-E7 — no tools folder without a home
- **Given** kvman with no `KVMAN_HOME`
- **When** `check` runs
- **Then** `narration.toolsFolder` is `null`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/setup.test.ts`

### M5.13-E8 — a run that isn't whole words of the line fails, and says why
- **Given** the line "والفريق الطبي يقدر يوثّق"
- **When** `ask record` is asked with `stress: ["الفريق الطبي"]`, then with `["الطبي والفريق"]`
- **Then** each fails `VALIDATION_FAILED`, the message saying the words aren't found as whole words of the line, as written
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/ask-record.test.ts`

### M5.13-E9 — a run matches across any spacing of the line
- **Given** the line "Open  the notes" (two spaces)
- **When** `ask record` is asked with `stress: ["Open the"]`, and its parts are made
- **Then** the question is asked, and "Open" and "the" are bold
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/ask-record.test.ts`, `extensions/kvcoder/test/web/record-parts.test.ts`
