# QA 33 — the lead agent's prompt and a worker's, and no welcome (ADR 0022, 1 to 4 and 9)

Asked: "remove the welcome note entirely", and "make the main agent as the big brain that decide, plans and execute", an expert "with a long long experience" that plans around the available connectors, runs work in parallel, delivers work "ready for production", and works "based on facts and evidence". Decided in ADR 0022; plan 08 §8.1, §8.2, §8.5, and §8.7. This file is the contract; every scenario's test name starts with its id. Unit tests call `buildPrompt`; the others run a test kernel with the fake model.

It retires M2.4-H8's welcome session, M2.4-E52, QA9-H1, QA9-E1, and QA20-E8, whose subject is removed. M2.4-H8's other half stays: notes and notices never reach the model.

## Happy path

- **QA33-H1 The lead's identity.** *Given* `role: 'lead'`, *then* the prompt starts "You are kvman Coder, the lead engineer on this work: a software engineer with long, deep experience who owns the outcome. You understand the request, decide, plan, and execute it, in the folder /w on Linux.", on each OS with its name, and it counts no years. `extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/prompt-lead.test.ts`
- **QA33-H2 The lead's six steps.** *Then* the method opens with "How you work. You are the one who decides: connectors and workers are how you act, and the result is yours.", scales to the task ("skip steps 3 and 5 and just do it"), and has the lines `1. Understand.`, `2. Clarify.`, `3. Plan.`, `4. Execute.`, `5. Delegate.`, and `6. Finish.` in that order. `extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/prompt-lead.test.ts`
- **QA33-H3 The plan is built from what the agent has, and names what runs together.** *Then* step 3 says the plan holds "which steps can run at the same time" and "Plan only with the connectors and workers you have.", and step 1 says "Work out what the person needs, not only what they typed." `extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/prompt-lead.test.ts`
- **QA33-H4 Parallel work.** *Then* step 4 says "running the independent ones together", step 5 says "Several `delegate run` calls in one reply run in parallel." and "Read what a worker returns and check it before you rely on it: its work is your responsibility.", and the rules say to put independent calls (reads, searches, separate files, separate workers) in one reply and calls that depend on each other in separate replies. `extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/prompt-lead.test.ts`
- **QA33-H5 Ready for production.** *Then* step 6 is "6. Finish. The work is done when it is ready for production: it does what was asked, it handles the errors and edge cases that will happen, it follows the project's conventions, its checks and tests pass, and nothing unfinished or temporary is left behind." `extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/prompt-lead.test.ts`
- **QA33-H6 Evidence.** *Then* both roles have "Decide from evidence: every decision and every claim rests on something you read, ran, or were told. When you report a result, say what you checked and what you didn't." `extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/prompt-lead.test.ts`
- **QA33-H7 A worker's identity and method.** *Given* `role: 'worker'`, *then* the prompt starts "You are kvman Coder, working on one task that the lead agent gave you, in the folder /w on Linux: a software engineer with long, deep experience.", the method opens "How you work. Do the task you were given and return the result; don't re-plan it or widen it, and ask the person only if you are blocked.", and has the lines `1. Understand.`, `2. Do it.`, and `3. Return.` `extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/prompt-worker.test.ts`
- **QA33-H8 Both roles keep the tool, the connectors, and the closing rule.** *Then* each role's prompt has the paragraph about `run`, the paragraph about connectors with `risky`, and ends its method with the call-or-final-answer rule. `extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/prompt-worker.test.ts`
- **QA33-H9 A chat's prompt is the lead's and a subagent's is the worker's.** *Given* a chat whose agent runs `delegate run`, *then* `kvcoder.prompt.get` of the chat has "the lead engineer on this work" and of the child has "working on one task that the lead agent gave you", followed by its `## Worker: <name>` block. `extensions/kvcoder/test/prompt-roles.test.ts`
- **QA33-H10 A new workspace has no session.** *Given* a test kernel with the default settings, *then* Home has no session and a workspace opened later has none. `extensions/kvcoder/test/no-welcome.test.ts`
- **QA33-H11 The Chats card has one setting.** *Then* `kvcoder.ui.get`'s configuration has the Chats card with `kvcoder.sessions.keep` alone. `extensions/kvcoder/test/ui.test.ts`
- **QA33-H12 The workers setting is typed.** *Then* `kvcoder.delegate.workers` is a key of kvcoder's `Settings`, read with its type. `extensions/kvcoder/test/no-welcome.test.ts`

## Edge cases

- **QA33-E1 A worker has no plan and no delegate step.** *Then* a worker's prompt has none of "Write the plan as the artifact `plan`", "`ask confirm`", "`delegate`", "6. Finish.", "Keep replies short", and "the lead engineer on this work". `extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/prompt-worker.test.ts`
- **QA33-E2 The lead's prompt has nothing of a worker's.** *Then* it has neither "that the lead agent gave you" nor "3. Return.", and no longer "If another agent gave you your task". `extensions/kvcoder/test/unit/prompt-lead.test.ts`
- **QA33-E3 The setting is gone.** *Then* `kernel.settings.list` has no `kvcoder.welcome`, and setting it fails as an unknown key does. `extensions/kvcoder/test/no-welcome.test.ts`
- **QA33-E4 The texts are gone.** *Then* neither catalog has `kvcoder.welcome.title`, `kvcoder.welcome.default`, `kvcoder.welcome.description`, or `kvcoder.ui.welcomeChat`. `extensions/kvcoder/test/locales.test.ts`
- **QA33-E5 A title is a string.** *Then* `kvcoder.session.list` and `kvcoder.session.get` answer `title` as a string, and the session list and the conversation header show it as it is, or "New chat" for an empty one. `extensions/kvcoder/test/web/sessions.test.ts`
- **QA33-E6 A note still shows and never reaches the model.** *Given* a note added with `kvcoder.note.add`, *then* the conversation shows it translated, and the model's request holds neither it nor a notice. `extensions/kvcoder/test/no-welcome.test.ts`, `extensions/kvcoder/test/web/conversation-messages.test.ts`
