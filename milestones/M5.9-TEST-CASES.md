# M5.9 — Reviewing a video, and images the model sees: test cases

Read: `16` §16.10; `08` §8.4; `07` (a model's `input`); ADR 0115, 20, 46 to 48.

The layers: kvcoder's checks of a result's images, the messages a model is sent, and the live step's progress as unit tests; a registered command with `images: true` through the test kernel with the fake model; the call card's thumbnails and the activity line's progress in `happy-dom`; `review`'s parsing and sheet plan as unit tests; and `review` end to end on a real video made with the ffmpeg on the PATH.

## Happy path

### M5.9-H1 — a command with `images: true` shows the model its images
- **Given** a test extension's connector command with `images: true` that answers two PNG files of the workspace, a model that takes images, and one that doesn't
- **When** the agent calls it and the next step is sent
- **Then** for the first model the tool result holds its text, then the two images; for the second, the text ends with `[2 images not shown: this model doesn't take images]`; the result's details keep the images' ids and names
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/result-images.test.ts`

### M5.9-H2 — the call card shows the images as thumbnails that open at full size
- **Given** a stored result with two images in its details
- **When** its call card is opened
- **Then** it shows two thumbnails, each a link to `/api/files/<id>?workspaceId=<workspace>` that opens in a new tab
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/web/call-images.test.ts`

### M5.9-H3 — a running call shows its extension's newest progress, translated
- **Given** a running `video compose` call, owned by `@kvman/kvvideo` with the namespace `kvvideo`
- **When** the stream brings `{ key: 'kvvideo.progress.scene', params: { n: 1, of: 3 } }` then `{ n: 2, of: 3 }` from `@kvman/kvvideo`
- **Then** the call shows "Scene 2 of 3"
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/web/live-step.test.ts`, `extensions/kvcoder/test/web/call-images.test.ts`

### M5.9-H4 — `review` measures the video and finds its problems
- **Given** ffmpeg's analysis output with a silence of 4 s, a black picture of 1 s, a frozen picture of 7 s, and a loudness of −20 LUFS with a true peak of −0.5 dBTP
- **When** it is read
- **Then** `problems` has, in time order, the silence, the black picture, the frozen picture, and the loudness at 0 for the whole video; `loudness` is `{ integratedLufs: -20, truePeakDb: -0.5 }`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/review-analysis.test.ts`

### M5.9-H5 — the frames of the sheets
- **Given** a video sidecar with three scenes and four captions
- **When** the frames are planned
- **Then** they are the scenes' middles, 400 ms after each transition, and each caption's middle, in time order, none within 500 ms of an earlier one; without a sidecar, one every 5 s; past 96, 96 spread evenly; 12 to a sheet, at most 8 sheets
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/unit/review-frames.test.ts`

### M5.9-H6 — a real review: sheets the model sees, and the problems found
- **Given** the ffmpeg on the PATH, and a video of 2 s of colour, 1 s of black, and 4 s of silence at the end
- **When** `review` runs
- **Then** it reports the black picture near 2000 ms and the silence; its `images` are JPEG sheets in the kernel's files, each 3 frames wide; progress gave `kvvideo.progress.sheet`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/real-review.test.ts`

### M5.9-H7 — the connector's `review` lets the model see its images
- **Given** kvvideo's `video` connector
- **When** it is listed
- **Then** `review` runs `kvvideo.video.review` with `images: true`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/registration.test.ts`

### M5.9-H8 — the newest 24 sheets are kept
- **Given** 24 sheets kept
- **When** a review makes 2 more
- **Then** the 2 oldest are deleted with their files
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/review.test.ts`

## Edge cases

### M5.9-E1 — wrong images fail the call
- **When** the command answers 9 images, a text file, a PNG over 5 MiB, or an id that isn't a file
- **Then** the call fails `VALIDATION_FAILED` and nothing is shown to the model as an image
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/result-images.test.ts`

### M5.9-E2 — a cleared result loses its images
- **Given** a result with images at or before the chat's cleared point
- **When** the next step is sent
- **Then** the result is the cleared line, with no images
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/result-images.test.ts`

### M5.9-E3 — `images` from a command without `images: true` is plain JSON
- **When** a command without `images: true` answers `images`
- **Then** the model reads them in the JSON text, and no image is sent
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/result-images.test.ts`

### M5.9-E4 — progress from another extension, outside the namespace, or of another shape is ignored
- **When** the stream brings a chunk from `@kvman/kvbuilder`, one from `@kvman/kvvideo` with the key `kvcoder.x`, and one with a `params` holding an object
- **Then** the running call shows no progress
- Test: `extensions/kvcoder/test/web/live-step.test.ts`

### M5.9-E5 — `review` of a file that isn't an MP4, doesn't exist, or is outside the workspace
- **Then** `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `NOT_FOUND`, and `VALIDATION_FAILED`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/review.test.ts`

### M5.9-E6 — a video without sound
- **When** a video with no sound stream is reviewed
- **Then** `loudness` is `null` and there is no silence problem
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/real-review.test.ts`

### M5.9-E7 — check asks for drawtext and xstack
- **When** ffmpeg lacks them
- **Then** `check`'s `missing` ends with `drawtext` and `xstack`
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/setup.test.ts`
