# M5.11 — The production guide, and the whole video by hand: test cases

Read: `16` the opening, §16.1, §16.12; ADR 0115, 1, 5, 6, 13, 23, 52, and 55 to 58.

The layers: the guide and the connector's entries through the test kernel, read back from the chat's prompt; and the by-hand check, part of it as an end-to-end test with Chrome and the ffmpeg on the PATH, and the part only the owner can do (the services with their keys, a voice recorded on each host) as a list in the README.

## Happy path

### M5.11-H1 — after `/video`, the prompt holds the guide's seven groups and its steps
- **Given** a chat after `/video`
- **When** its prompt is read
- **Then** its "Making videos" section has, in order, Story, Look, Motion, Text, Sound, Accuracy and licences, Review, The steps, and Rules; it says there is no music and never to generate images, and names `video check`, `ask record`, `voice`, `compose`, and `review` in its steps
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/registration.test.ts`

### M5.11-H2 — the connector's description and its commands, each with an example
- **Given** kvvideo started
- **When** the `video` connector is listed
- **Then** its description is the plan's, and `check`, `fonts`, `brand`, `brand-set`, `record`, `narrate`, `voice`, `compose`, and `review` each have one example whose input its command takes
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/registration.test.ts`

### M5.11-H3 — the whole video by hand, with what can run here
- **Given** Chrome and the ffmpeg on the PATH, a local website, an HTML title page in Arabic, an image of the person's, and a tone as the narration
- **When** a title page, a website in a window, the same website on a phone, and the image are recorded and composed with the camera, a callout, a highlight, a blur, every transition, sound effects, and Arabic and English captions, once in each caption style
- **Then** each video is made, its frames are saved for a person to look at, and `review` of it finds no black picture; `review` of a video with a planted silence and a black second finds both
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/whole-video.test.ts`; the planted silence and black second in `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/real-review.test.ts`; an Arabic callout in `extensions/kvvideo/test/e2e/drawn-images.test.ts`

## By hand, by the owner

### M5.11-H4 — the services and the voice on each host
- **When** the owner has the agent use a licensed image from the web in a video, narrates once with ElevenLabs and once with OpenAI (`tone` and `speed`), and records a scene with `ask record` in the browser, the desktop app on each OS, and VS Code
- **Then** the image is saved in the video's folder with its source and licence in the script, each narration plays with its captions in time, and each host records or shows its fallback
- Recorded in: `README.md` (the by-hand list)
