# M5.16 — The guide for videos that move, and the KVCare promotion made again: test cases

Read: `16` §16.1; ADR 0120.

The layers: the guide in the prompt after `/video`, through the test kernel; the KVCare promotion made again with `/video`, by hand with the owner.

## Happy path

### M5.16-H1 — the guide names what makes a video move
- **Given** a chat after `/video`
- **When** its prompt is read
- **Then** its guide names `kvvideo/brand.css`, `kvvideo/motion.css`, `kvvideo/lib/three/`, `data-sound`, `video narration-set`, `preview.mp4`, re-timing the treatment from the voice, `kv-drift`, `maxSteps` of at least 30, and never processing a video with `shell`; and keeps its ten stages, its production rules, and its rules in order
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/registration.test.ts`

### M5.16-H2 — the person's own voice needs no service, and a generated one is set up after asking
- **Given** a chat after `/video`
- **When** its prompt is read
- **Then** it says "The person's own voice needs no service.", that a narration service is needed only for a generated voice, to offer Piper or a service with `ask choice`, to install Piper and its voices in `toolsFolder`, never in the workspace, and to set the service with `video narration-set`, never asking the person to edit settings
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/registration.test.ts`

### M5.16-H3 — by hand: the KVCare promotion made again
- **Given** the owner's KVCare workspace and the request "promotional video about kvcare in Arabic", in their own voice, 16:9
- **When** it is made with `/video`
- **Then** no API key is asked for; the treatment shows a moving preview; the scenes move at every moment in the app's font and colours, with its logo; the sound has effects; the captions are even (or off where the text is on screen); and `review` reports no problem
- Test: by hand (README)

## Edge cases

### M5.16-E1 — the guide no longer limits scenes to CSS or examples to a service
- **Given** a chat after `/video`
- **When** its prompt is read
- **Then** it doesn't say "CSS animations and transitions only", "never script timers", or "whose examples are generated", and shows no style frame as an HTML artifact
- Test: `extensions/kvvideo/test/registration.test.ts`
