`video compose` frames a recorded website in a window or a phone on a gradient, colour, or image background, zooms the camera smoothly to each click and typing (and an image slowly), lays callouts, highlights, and blurs on a recording, and burns captions in as boxed, outline, or word-by-word highlight, or none, from the narration's sidecar; 1920 × 1080 by default. `video brand` and `brand-set` keep the brand's logo, colours, and font. `check` asks for the filters these need (ADR 0115).
