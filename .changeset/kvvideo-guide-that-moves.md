The production guide (ADR 0120): your own voice needs no narration service; a generated one is offered as Piper or a service and set with `video narration-set`, installed outside the workspace; `style.css` imports `kvvideo/brand.css` and `kvvideo/motion.css`; the treatment shows a moving `preview.mp4`; the treatment is re-timed from the recorded voice; scenes move at every moment with the kit, scripts, and three.js, with sound cues; captions are off when the words are on screen; designer helpers get at least 30 steps; and a video is never post-processed with `shell`.
