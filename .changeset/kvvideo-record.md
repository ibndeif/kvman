`video record`: records a page of an app on this computer in the installed Chrome or Edge, with click, type, press, hover, scroll, wait, waitFor, and goto steps, a drawn cursor, and each step's time; kvvideo captures the page's frames itself and encodes them as H.264 with the ffmpeg on the PATH (ADR 0107, 25). kvman's own address is never recorded or reached.
