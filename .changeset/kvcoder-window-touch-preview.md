A long chat keeps a window of 200 messages on the page, with "Show earlier messages" above it (`kvcoder.message.list` takes `beforeSeq`). On touch devices kvcoder's controls have a touch area of at least 44 px, and keyboard focus is always visible. The artifact connector tells the model how to build a page whose buttons work inside the preview.
