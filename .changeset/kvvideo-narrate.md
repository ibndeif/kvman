`video narrate`: narrates a scene's text into an MP3 with ElevenLabs, OpenAI, or Piper on this computer, as the person chooses in kvvideo's configuration, and answers its length and its captions, timed in proportion to their length. The services' keys are kvvideo's secrets `elevenlabs.apiKey` and `openai.apiKey`.
