Every video is made through a production workflow of ten stages: a brief, research, a concept (debated by the council when it is open), a treatment with style frames the person agrees to, a production map, the voice first, the pictures (designers build HTML scenes in parallel), review rounds, and delivery. `video look` shows the model a page's frames at chosen moments; a page's animations wait for its recording to start, so they keep time with the narration; `compose` no longer writes an `.srt` (ADR 0117).
