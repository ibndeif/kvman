kvcoder gives its configuration: its settings in four cards and the `kvcoder.connectors` component, a list of every connector with a switch. The new setting `kvcoder.connectors.disabled` names the connectors that are off: one is left out of the prompt and can't be called, from the next step. `kvcoder.connector.list` rows gain `enabled` (ADR 0014).
