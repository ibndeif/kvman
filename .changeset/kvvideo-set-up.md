kvvideo: a new bundled extension that makes videos of the person's app. `/video` turns on the `video` connector in a chat, with `check` (what is ready: ffmpeg, the installed browser, narration) and `fonts` (the bundled caption fonts), its guide, and its configuration of narration, recording, and programs (ADR 0107).
