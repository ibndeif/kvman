The thinking pickers list the chosen model's own levels, up to Extra high and Max where it has them, and show Off greyed for a model that doesn't think. A chat, a new chat, and a helper always keep a level their model has: a level the model lacks moves to the nearest one, the next higher, else the next lower (ADR 0087, 11).
