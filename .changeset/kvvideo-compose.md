`video compose`: joins scenes of recorded clips or images, with their narrations and burned-in captions, into an H.264 and AAC MP4; captions use a bundled font or a .ttf or .otf of the workspace, and each line reads in its own direction, so Arabic reads right to left. The chat shows the video in a player card; the newest 10 videos of a workspace keep a copy for it. Tajawal is no longer bundled: its bold alef didn't render.
