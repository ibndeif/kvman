A fourth built-in guide, `conventions`, states kvman's rules for an extension in one place: names, public and private, errors, jobs and what ends with them, handler points in place of events, storage, settings and their scopes, secrets, validation, text for people, who owns UI, the docs pair, dependencies, and checks. `kvman-docs`, `docs list` and `docs get`, and the scaffold's copy into a project's `docs/` now have four guides, and kvbuilder's `building` page names it (ADR 0030).
