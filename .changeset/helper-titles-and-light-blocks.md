`delegate run` takes a required `title`, the role the run plays ("UI expert", "Node.js expert"): the helper's card, the job list, and the finished-helper card show it, with the first line of its task under the title (ADR 0037). A long output shows its first 12 lines, a line of dots, and its last 5. A command's and an output's blocks follow the theme: light in the light theme, dark in the dark one.
