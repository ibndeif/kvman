# ADR 0066 — What M1.6 proves, and what moves to M1.7 and M1.8

- **Status**: accepted
- **Date**: 2026-09-25
- **Milestone**: M1.6
- **Decided by**: the product owner

## Question

M1.6's Done-when names "`onAbort` on deadline" and "a reply … reaches … a late HTTP caller", but failing awaiting messages at their deadline is M1.7's Build list and `GET /messages/:id` is M1.8's. `ctx.message` is in `05` §5.4 but not in M1.6's Build list, although the prompt pattern (`02` §2.8) needs the deferred command's id.

## Options

1. **M1.6 builds the awaiting deadline and the kernel reply read**; M1.7 and M1.8 build the rest.
2. **Move the bullets**: "onAbort on deadline" to M1.7, "late HTTP caller" to M1.8.

And for `ctx.message`: include it in M1.6, or leave it for M1.7.

## Decision

Option 2, and `ctx.message` is included:

- M1.6 records `defer({ onAbort })` with the deferred command and checks that `onAbort` is an internal command of the deferring extension (`VALIDATION_FAILED` otherwise). M1.7 ends awaiting commands at their deadline and sends `onAbort` `{ commandId, reason }`; its Done-when proves "onAbort on deadline".
- M1.6 proves that a stored reply reaches `ctx.command` and a continuation. M1.8 proves the late HTTP caller.
- M1.6 adds `ctx.message` (the current message, read-only). `ctx.locale` and `ctx.i18n` stay in M2.11; `ctx.signal` and `ctx.deadlineAt` stay in M1.7.

## Consequences

`15` M1.6, M1.7, and M1.8 are corrected.
