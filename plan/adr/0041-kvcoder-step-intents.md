# ADR 0041 — durable kvcoder step intents and generation claims

Status: accepted by the product owner through confirmation of the data contract, following ADR0040. This records KVC-02/03 contracts before source, not completion of their implementation.

## Stored contracts

1. Add internal session fields `stepGeneration` (nonnegative safe integer, legacy read default0) and `stepIntentId` (string|null, legacy read defaultnull). Generation increases once per newly prepared intent for the session lifetime, not once per turn; no wrap or reset. A next value outside the safe integer range fails validation before effects and rolls the transaction back. This is a numeric representation boundary, not a task budget or step cap. Keep existing turnId/stepJobId and public session/turn DTOs and statuses/outcomes.
2. Add the workspace collection `step-intents`, exposed internally as `stepIntents` in records/txRecords. Its kernel-generated ID is the intent identity. Required document fields: sessionId/turnId strings; positive safe-integer generation; phase prepared|claimed|settled|invalidated|needs-review; nullable-string jobId/legacyJobId/reason; ISO createdAt/updatedAt strings. New records explicitly write all fields, including null. No new database table, raw SQL or Home index. reason is a known diagnostic code, never a payload, output or secret.
3. prepared means intent and running state committed together, without a winner allowed to execute effects. claimed means one short synchronous transaction validated session/turn/current-intent/generation and bound the winning jobId before any effect. settled means guarded step results and waiting/end/next-intent state committed together. invalidated forbids further execution/publication, including cancellation. needs-review retains an unconfirmed claim whose turn ends interrupted with the approved notice and no automatic replay.
4. jobId may name a published candidate while prepared; after claim it is authoritative. Late publishers cannot replace that winner or a newer intent/generation. Losing/invalid candidates do not execute effects and are cancelled when known. Kernel enqueue and cancellation stay outside store transactions. Crash-safe claim is the execution dedupe boundary; do not promise zero duplicate candidate rows or an atomic SQLite+kernel enqueue operation.

## Execution and compatibility

5. The private step input becomes `{ sessionId, turnId, intentId, generation }`. The old `{ sessionId, turnId }` shape remains only for an exact verified binding to the saved legacy job: command name, workspace, input identity and valid turn must match before atomic claim. A schema default is not evidence of unexecuted work. Unprovable legacy effects are not replayed; a legitimate question/child wait is not interrupted solely because the new fields are missing. Enumerate old active records completely through after; do not filter missing fields as JSON null.
6. Each publication and state/result/end/failure/cancel transition validates its expected turn, intent and generation where it derives from a step; stale writers cannot replace or finish the current one. Claim precedes approved-call effects, model work and ordinary tool effects. A successor intent is prepared in the state transaction, not after it.
7. Follow keeps its existing public shape and becomes only a hint: authoritative current-state reread and workspace/session/job stream ownership reject stale follow and live publication. Do not add public generation fields to DTOs for this part. Existing public cancel input is not silently broadened; displayed-turn binding belongs to the selected UI/cancel contract in KVC-21.
8. Intents remain with their turn for diagnostics. Never prune active intents or uncertain-shutdown tracking; deletion follows confirmed owned cleanup with the turn/session under KVC-05/31. Keep-all adds no hidden expiry. Receipt, parent-start-permit and deleting-state fields for KVC-01/04–06 are not approved by this ADR and require their own presentation before source.

## Recovery policy already approved in ADR0040

Later authorized positive send/answer commands reconcile safe intents for all sessions of their own workspace. Stop/delete never dispatch; closed-workspace policy and kernel queued-job recovery remain. Inspect known jobs; bounded jobs.list or missing job metadata is not a replay permit for claimed effects. Terminal/missing claimed jobs without settlement end interrupted with an explicit unknown-effects notice and retained uncertain-shutdown tracking. Continue only by a fresh explicit request, without replaying the old step or approval. No cross-workspace SDK override, Home-wide startup launch, new public paused/review status or dedicated resume command.
