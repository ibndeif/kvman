# ADR NNNN — Title in a few words

- **Status**: proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR NNNN
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Milestone**: the milestone that raised the question (e.g. M1.4)
- **Decided by**: the product owner

## Question

What the plan did not specify, or which plan sections seemed to disagree. Quote or cite the sections (`02` §2.4).

## Options

1. **Option one** — what it means and its consequences.
2. **Option two** — what it means and its consequences.

## Decision

The option chosen, stated as a rule that code and tests can follow.

## Consequences

What changes in the code, the tests, and the plan. Name every plan section corrected by this decision.

## Measurements

Only for decisions backed by numbers (spikes, benchmarks): the method, the machine, and the results.
