# The libraries kvvideo serves to scenes

kvvideo serves these libraries, unchanged, at a page's `kvvideo/lib/` address (plan 16 §16.5; ADR 0120, 6 and 30). Each
is a dependency of kvvideo, installed with its own licence file.

| Library | Version | Address | Licence | Copyright |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js) | 0.187.0 | `kvvideo/lib/three/build/`, `kvvideo/lib/three/examples/jsm/` | MIT | © 2010-2026 three.js authors |
| [anime.js](https://github.com/juliangarnier/anime) | 4.5.0 | `kvvideo/lib/anime.js` | MIT | © 2025 Julian Garnier |
| [lottie-web](https://github.com/airbnb/lottie-web) | 5.13.0 | `kvvideo/lib/lottie.js` | MIT | © 2015 Bodymovin |

The motion kit, `motion.css`, is kvvideo's own, under kvvideo's MIT licence.
