# Social clips

Read this at the concept of a social clip: 1080 × 1920, watched on a phone, often on mute.

## The first second

- Something moves in the first frame, and words are on screen within half a second. No logo, no title card, no build-up.
- The voice starts at once: the first scene's narration begins 300 ms in.
- Promise the payoff now: show the result first, ask the question, or name the mistake.

## Pace

- The picture changes every 1 to 3 seconds: a new element, a cut, a camera move. Nothing holds longer than 3 seconds.
- Shorter sentences, and a `speed` up to about 1.1.
- Use `cut` between most scenes, and keep any other transition short.

## Shapes

- **15 seconds**: the hook (1 s), one line of context, the tip, then the result or the call to action.
- **30 seconds**: the hook, the problem, the solution in quick steps, the result, the call to action.
- **60 seconds**: the result first, "here's how, in N steps", three to five steps of about 8 seconds, the result again, the call to action, and an ending that leads back into the opening so it plays again.

## Words on screen

- Captions on, since most people watch on mute; keep sentences short so the caption lines stay short.
- Other on-screen text sits in the top 40% of the frame, three to five words at a time, large and bold, entering fast.
- Keep everything out of the bottom 20% and the top 10% of the frame, where the apps draw their buttons and names.
